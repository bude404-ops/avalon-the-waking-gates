The World

A dark-fantasy action RPG set in the Ancient Mythic Age — a primordial world shaped by dead gods, where megalithic monuments dwarf everything mortals build and magic hums in every stone.

You wake in one of six realms, each bound to one of six classes — Sovereign, Ravager, Warden, Veilborn, Weaver, Wildborn — and one patron deity. You learn the other gods' powers by traveling their realms. No loot treadmills, no gear scores, no daily chores — progression is mastery, story, exploration, and divine connection, and it's permanent: your victories literally reshape the map.

The Two Scales

The endgame is the Two Scales: you start as a mortal hero and finish commanding primordial gods in world-shaping battles against the Unmaking — the force unraveling existence itself.

The Goal

Uncover the truth behind the Waking Gates, walk all Six Paths, gather the pantheon's powers, and decide how the world ends — or begins again.

Built for real life: 20–30 minute sessions, and every single one moves your story forward.
