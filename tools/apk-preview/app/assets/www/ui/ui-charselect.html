AEDAN STORMCROWN — SOVEREIGN (M) • SKYREND

Born under the storm marches where the standing stones drink lightning. The Sovereign does not beg the storm for permission — he plants his banner in the high terraces and the gate opens to him. First hero of the Twelve, keeper of the way-gate lantern.
