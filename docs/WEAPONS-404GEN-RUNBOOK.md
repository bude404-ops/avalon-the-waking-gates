# 404-GEN WEAPON RUNBOOK — Unity Seat Paste-Prompts

**Status:** Weapon canon plates rolled (art/pending/WEAPON-*-V1.png). These are the
Stage A reference plates per the Clean-Plate Law: single weapon, flat dark-neutral
background, faint featureless grey silhouette for scale-read, small weapons on the
ground. Sovereign's spear is already built as a real 3D prop
(models/generated/SPEAR-SOVEREIGN-PROP.glb) — 5 remain.

**How to use on the Unity seat:** open 404-GEN, feed it the plate image as the
reference, paste the matching prompt below, generate the 3D weapon, then QC
side-by-side against the plate (kit-integrity: no extra weapons, no script, no runes).

**Prompt law (applies to every roll):** knotwork + La Tène spiral PATTERNS only —
no deity names, no titles, never the word 'elven', no letters/runes/glyphs in output.
Leaf-shaped blades only. Engraved bronze / dark iron / leather / carved wood.
Cold desaturated, weathered, ancient.

## QC checklist per weapon (before it becomes canon)
1. Silhouette matches the plate at a glance (leaf-blade law, size read)
2. Zero extra weapons/parts (kit-integrity)
3. Zero letters/runes/glyphs anywhere in the texture
4. Materials: engraved bronze / dark iron / leather / carved wood only
5. Muted palette — no bright saturated colors
6. Renders clean on flat dark-neutral background

Verdicts land with Big, one weapon at a time, same protocol as class art.
