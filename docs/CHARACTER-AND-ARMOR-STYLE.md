# CHARACTER & ARMOR STYLE — THE MYTHIC AGE CRAFT GRAMMAR (proposal for Big's gate · Sept 8 2026)

**The question:** how do the new player characters — the six classes — and their armor look under the new lore?

**The tier answer:** Camelot was the MORTAL tier — mail, heraldry, brooches. The classes are OLDER: the FIRST CRAFTS of the Ancient Mythic Age, from before guilds, before sigils, before steel. Craft that remembers being material. The gear should feel like it was made by a person with two hands, a fire, and a reason — not fitted by an armorer's guild.

## THE SIX MATERIAL LAWS (the new gear law for player classes)

1. **RAW FIRST, SHAPED SECOND** — hide with the hair still on, cord-cross lashing, unpolished aged bronze bands, bone fittings, dark wood shafts, river-stone weights. Every piece shows both the hand that made it and the material it was. No polish, no guild finishes.
2. **THE GATE RUNE IS THE ONLY MARK** — carved or burned into gear (the spear's single band, the hammer's cheek, the claw-frames), never embroidered, never a brooch, never a banner. One rune, one meaning. (Rune Origin Law: Avalon gate-rune script only.)
3. **NO UNIFORMS** — the age before guilds has no standardized kit. Each class's gear is a personal assemblage — the gear tells the character's story (a mended strap, a replaced toggle, a trophy-bone fitting).
4. **SILHOUETTE = IDENTITY** — the class must read at distance with zero heraldry: Sovereign's vertical spear-line and planted stance; Ravager's sheer mass and wide hang; Warden's low center and long planted haft; Veilborn's layered wraps and half-veil crouch; Weaver's long spare silhouette and staff; Wildborn's feral low line.
5. **TWO-LAYER LIGHT + CHISEL SIGNATURE** — the Carved World house style governs everything, unchanged: painterly oil-render, chiseled surfaces, cold ambient world, muted palette.
6. **NEUTRAL-START** — no glowing eyes, no flame effects in canon art. God attunement adds the flame-color edge in-engine only (existing law).

## WHAT DIES WITH THE PILGRIMS
Heraldry — order cloaks, round brooches, banner poles, the Order colorways as identity. Polished mail. The pilgrim frame (Wayfarer/Keeper/etc. silhouettes).

## WHAT CARRIES FORWARD (universal laws, all preserved)
The Hood (silhouette option — natural fit for the Veilborn), the Gate Rune as universal Mark, Flame-Color rule, Rune Origin, Environment-Typing (classes staged in their native mythic sites), Face-Distinctness, Neutral-Start, the Carved World style.

## COLOR
Deeper muted than Camelot: bone, umber, oxide reds, verdigris bronze, charcoal — with each class's single accent used like a whisper, never a banner.
