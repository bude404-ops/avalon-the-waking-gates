## THE MYTHIC CELTIC HUMAN VISUAL STYLE

Ancient human warrior craftsmanship inspired by Celtic and Arthurian fantasy — strictly human craft, never otherworldly refined. **Legendary through craftsmanship, symbols, and imposing silhouettes.**

### MATERIALS
Bronze, dark iron, leather, engraved knotwork, spirals, torcs, cloaks, layered battle cloth, practical articulated armor.

### FEMALE SILHOUETTE
Athletic, elegant, fitted armor emphasizing the waist and feminine silhouette; tasteful exposed shoulders/arms or upper back; layered battle-skirts, fitted leggings, ornate cloaks and jewelry.

### MALE SILHOUETTE
Muscular, broad-shouldered, powerful silhouette; fitted SOLID chest armor emphasizing the torso (full-coverage breastplate, no chest cut per the SOLID-BREASTPLATE LAW); exposed or sleeveless arms; heavier shoulders, belts, battle-kilt panels, bracers, and large cloaks.

### WEAPONS
Celtic-inspired swords, spears, greatswords, warhammers, axes, bows, and daggers. Distinctive LEAF-SHAPED BLADES, engraved bronze/dark iron, Celtic knotwork, spiral motifs, leather-wrapped grips, carved wood, clan symbols, ceremonial detailing. Weapons read: ancient, handcrafted, brutal, elegant, legendary.

### UNIVERSAL READ
Both genders: sexy, heroic, battle-ready, distinctly human, culturally consistent.

### LAW RECONCILIATIONS
- CLOAKS + JEWELRY ARE IN: the old no-cloak/no-brooch/no-jewelry ban on class art is superseded — ornate cloaks, torcs, jewelry are canon for classes.
- FITTED ARMOR IS IN: the no-polished-metal / raw-only rule is superseded — bronze and dark iron armor pieces are canon; raw hide/cord stays as trim and under-layers.
- CLAN SYMBOLS = knotwork/spiral motifs + Avalon gate-rune marks ONLY — Rune Origin Law holds, no real-world scripts (no ogham, no futhark).
- Kit-Integrity, Fit-Body, hourglass sizing, Female Face/Expression/Scene laws all hold unchanged.
- Split v2: FEMALES 50/50 functional-provocative (Sept 8 ~8:45 PM, Big's ratio order); males stay 70/30. Realized through this grammar.

## THE OLD FIRST CRAFTS GRAMMAR (below — partially superseded)

# CHARACTER & ARMOR STYLE — THE MYTHIC AGE CRAFT GRAMMAR (proposal for Big's gate · Sept 8 2026)

**The question:** how do the new player characters — the six classes — and their armor look under the new lore?

**The tier answer:** Camelot was the MORTAL tier — mail, heraldry, brooches. The classes are OLDER: the FIRST CRAFTS of the Ancient Mythic Age, from before guilds, before sigils, before steel. Craft that remembers being material. The gear should feel like it was made by a person with two hands, a fire, and a reason — not fitted by an armorer's guild.

## THE SIX MATERIAL LAWS (the new gear law for player classes)

1. **RAW FIRST, SHAPED SECOND** — hide with the hair still on, cord-cross lashing, unpolished aged bronze bands, bone fittings, dark wood shafts, river-stone weights. Every piece shows both the hand that made it and the material it was. No polish, no guild finishes.
2. **THE GATE RUNE IS THE ONLY MARK** — carved or burned into gear (the spear's single band, the hammer's cheek, the claw-frames), never embroidered, never a brooch, never a banner. One rune, one meaning. (Rune Origin Law: Avalon gate-rune script only.)
3. **NO UNIFORMS** — the age before guilds has no standardized kit. Each class's gear is a personal assemblage — the gear tells the character's story (a mended strap, a replaced toggle, a trophy-bone fitting).
4. **SILHOUETTE = IDENTITY** — the class must read at distance with zero heraldry: Sovereign's vertical spear-line and planted stance; Ravager's sheer mass and wide hang; Warden's low center and long planted haft; Veilborn's layered wraps and half-veil crouch; Weaver's long spare silhouette and staff; Wildborn's feral low line.
5. **TWO-LAYER LIGHT + CHISEL SIGNATURE** — the Carved World house style governs everything, unchanged: painterly oil-render, chiseled surfaces, cold ambient world, muted palette.
6. **NEUTRAL-START** — no glowing eyes, no flame effects in canon art. God attunement adds the flame-color edge in-engine only (existing law).
