# CREATURE-BIBLE — D&D-derived creature system for AVALON: The Waking Gates
(Bude's Sept 14 2026 research directive: "research how D&D has and utilizes creatures and
such as we can pull inspiration from it for mounts and other world creatures, like using
griffins for the sovereign")

## WHAT D&D GETS RIGHT (the research)
1. **TYPES ARE WORLDVIEW, not labels.** D&D sorts every creature into a type (beast /
   monstrosity / celestial / construct / elemental / fey / dragon...). The load-bearing
   split: BEASTS are natural animals; MONSTROSITIES are magical beings that don't obey
   natural biology. The type tells the player what the creature IS in the world's
   physics — before a single stat is read.
   -> AVALON: Bude's mythic-mount law ("not normal animals with region effects but more
   mythology") is EXACTLY this split. The v1 animal+glow mounts were beasts; the saddled
   mythic set are monstrosities. We adopt the two-tier read: natural fauna exists for
   world texture; MYTHIC BEINGS are the ones that matter (mounts, keystones, titans).
2. **CREATURES ARE DESIGNED BY FUNCTION FIRST.** D&D monsters have jobs — ambient
   texture, predator, bodyguard/keystone, apex boss. Skin follows role, never the
   reverse.
   -> AVALON per-realm creature roster, four functions per realm:
   a) AMBIENT FAUNA (living texture, never hostile by default)
   b) PREDATORS (encounter pressure)
   c) HAZARD-KEYSTONE (guards or embodies the mythic-pocket gate — the mount's ability
      answers it: ember-keystone guards the lava pass only the Ember-Bear can cross)
   d) REALM TITAN / WORLD BOSS (apex — the realm's pull quest)
3. **THREAT TIERS (the CR principle).** Every creature has a known threat band, so
   encounters scale and the world has a food chain.
   -> AVALON: threat tiers ride the Six-Campaign Arc — a realm's titan outranks its
   keystones outranks its predators. Difficulty geography: deeper = older = worse.
4. **MOUNTS ARE EARNED, AND THE EARNING IS A BOND.** D&D griffon lore: proud,
   semi-intelligent, sought by knightly orders — griffon knights swear oaths to mount
   AND order (Waterdeep's Griffon Cavalry = an institution, not a rental service). The
   paladin's Find Steed is the class-mount archetype: the mount is a bond, not a
   purchase.
   -> AVALON mounts are already quest-earned (knowledge = unlock). The D&D upgrade:
   the questline IS the bond — you don't buy the beast, you witness it, feed its flame,
   fight beside it once; the saddle is the oath made visible. (Celtic saddles already
   carry exactly this read.)
5. **CLASS-BONDED LEGENDS ABOVE REALM MOUNTS.** D&D gives special mounts by class
   (paladin steeds), separate from the world's horses.
   -> AVALON PROPOSAL (locks on Bude's ruling): TWO TIERS —
   TIER 1: the six realm riding beasts (any class, realm questline, traversal key).
   TIER 2: ONE class-bonded legend per class, woken by the class's own arc —
   the SOVEREIGN'S GRIFFIN is the flagship: flight, mountain-peak and sky dungeons,
   the griffon-cavalry prestige of a crowned king. (Supersedes the v1 no-flying law
   the way the storm-bull leap already gestured at.)
6. **FLIGHT IS BALANCED BY HAZARD, NOT BANNED.** D&D DM doctrine: flying mounts meet
   winds, aerial predators, and lose attack efficacy — flight is a new gauntlet, not a
   taxi.
   -> AVALON flight design: storm winds (Skyrend weather), sky predators, dismount-to-
   fight law unchanged. The Griffin's sky dungeons are gauntlets.
7. **THE LAIR IS THE BOSS.** D&D legendary/lair actions: apex creatures are strongest at
   home; the dungeon geometry participates in the fight (multi-phase, lair turns).
   -> AVALON: world bosses fight inside their mythic pockets where the colossal
   interior IS the mechanic — reinforces WORLD-AND-DUNGEON-LAW's primordial geometry.
8. **ECOLOGY ENFORCES REALM IDENTITY.** Every creature entry says where it lives and
   what it eats; the biome's beast list IS the biome's mythology.
   -> AVALON: per-realm creature design language (ember seams in Ashfall, stone-souled
   in Stoneheart...) under the FIRE-COLOR LAW — natural fire amber everywhere, realm
   aura on magic light only.

## ROSTER SEEDS (draft — art rolls only on Bude's go)
- SOVEREIGN — GRIFFIN (TIER 2 flagship): ember-barred plumage, stone-crowned beak,
  storm-tested wings; the crowned king's wing. NO OTHER class flies in tier 2 v1.
- Realm creature sets: designed function-first per the four roles above, one design
  language per realm, rolled as concept plates when Bude opens the world-creature pass.
