# MOUNT-CANON — AVALON Mounts Law (Big-approved Sept 13, 2026)

Status: **CONCEPT LOCKED — Stage C.** Greenlit by Big ("im green lighting the mounts
concept and agree with you", Sept 13 2026). Nothing is generated, rigged, or forged
until: (1) the Sovereign is ruled final-quality by Big, and (2) Stage B combat controls
are approved. This document locks the DESIGN so no drift happens between now and then.

## The Three Mount Laws

1. **NO MOUNTED COMBAT — v1.** To fight, you dismount. Mounts are travel only.
   This keeps the Stage B combat law completely intact and defers the most expensive
   animation problem in the game (rider attack blending) until it is actually wanted.
2. **SAME CONTROLS.** A mount does not add control schemes — the floating joystick
   (Stage A) steers the beast exactly as it steers the Sovereign. Only speed changes.
   Zero new control learning on mobile. Tap-to-walk targets the mount's destination
   the same way.
3. **ONE QUESTLINE PER MOUNT — knowledge is the unlock.** Same philosophy as the
   shrine-gate fast-travel law: you earn a mount by learning its ways in its realm.
   **The Wildborn earns their realm mount cheapest** — free class-identity hook.

## Realm-Native Beasts — one per realm, mythic Celtic fauna, never generic horses

Mounts ride on the Camelot-fauna canon already queued in the bestiary; they are
realm-native beasts of the six realms, stark and grim-dark, staged under the same
realm-neutral bestiary laws when their canon keys roll.

| Realm | Mount | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Ashfall | **Ash-Elk** | ember-hide elk, antlers of cooled slag that glow at the crowns |
| Stoneheart | **Boulder Ram** | boulder-hided ram, hooves of grey stone |
| Marenth | **Tide-Strider** | long-legged shore beast that walks the shallows |
| Duskmourn | **Gloam-Pooka** | the shapeshifting pooka consents to be ridden — earned, never tamed |
| Everbloom | **Briar-Fox** | great briar-coated fox (canon fauna ENEMY-FAUNA-EVERBLOOM-BRIAR-FOX) |
| Skyrend | **Storm-Hart** | thunder-horned stag; horns crack faint storm-light |

- The **Ravenkin remains the ONE unique herald creature** — mounts never touch its
  station or its Reliquary exclusivity.
- **NO FLYING MOUNTS — v1.** Ground traversal keeps the seamless-world law honest
  (exploring on foot/mount is the game; shrine-gates are the reward) and keeps
  mobile camera + streaming loads sane. Flight is a distant, deliberate decision.

## Travel tier order (unchanged world law, now complete)

1. **On foot** — Sovereign movement (Stage A).
2. **Mounted** — ~2.5–3× walk speed, unlocked per realm by questline.
3. **Shrine-gate fast travel** — discovered gates only (knowledge = unlock).

## Pipeline & cost notes (for the forge ledger)

- Quadruped rigs are the heaviest model/animation work after the classes themselves.
- CMU mocap carries equine/quadruped capture — the CMU ROOT LOCK law and the
  zero-human forge chain (404-GEN generate → blender rig → CI forge → sign → vault)
  apply unchanged.
- One mount forged at a time, realm by realm, ONLY after Big rules Stage C open.
- Mount canon keys stage under the same enemy/creature art laws: neutral staging,
  no script/runes, fire-color law (mortal fire amber only), realm aura on magic light only.
