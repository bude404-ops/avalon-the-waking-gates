## I. LORE — why sprites exist (and why the Hollow fears them)
When the gods walked Mythos in the Walking Age, their element POOLED behind them — little eddies of divine essence left in footprints, hearths, storms, petals, braziers, tidepools, crystal seams. Over ages those eddies woke: tiny living motes of a region's tension — SPRITES. They imprint on mortals the way mortals imprint on gods, and follow a sprite-scale vessel because a god in a small shape is the best thing a small god can imagine.

**DEEP TRUTH (deep-lore tie):** sprites cannot be hollowed. They are already almost nothing — pure witness with a wisp of element. The Hollow needs substance to erase and a sprite is mostly LOOKING. Where a sprite floats, Grimlights will not spawn (they are the Witness mechanic made pocket-sized). No one in-world knows this. The gate-finches are descended from them — that is why the finches never sleep.

**Naming:** sprites hum — always the region's festival verse, slightly wrong, like a child's rendition. A player with a sprite learns the Song faster (see abilities).

## III. ABILITIES + LEVELING (levels 1–10; utility-first, never power creep)
Base sprite earned via its region's festival questline (level 1). Levels via shared play (no sprite-specific grind — sprites grow by BEING WITH YOU, per the lore).

- **L1 PASSIVE — REGIONAL SENSE:** the sprite reacts to hidden things (unlit braziers nearby, buried Ledger-Stones, disguised Mimic Hollow) with its idle animation. Quality-of-life, teaches attention (recurring-motif training).
- **L3 PASSIVE — WITNESS WISP:** Grimlights will not spawn within 8 m of the sprite; in Witness Zones the sprite extends the radius by 4 m.
- **L3 ACTIVE — SMALL BOON (30 s, 3 min cooldown):** regional micro-buff — Cinderwisp: weapon glows warm (+fire tick on next hit) / Gustling: slow-fall / Bloomling: regen mote / Gloamflame: dark-sight + fear resist / Tidekin: swim speed + underwater breath / Pebbleheart: stagger resist. Cosmetic-adjacent power — helpful, never required.
- **L6 ACTIVE — HUM THE VERSE (per GAMEPLAY_DOCTRINE §5):** the sprite hums its region's festival verse — casting the shrine-buff AWAY from shrines. In the endgame rite, a max-level sprite harmonizes: +1 chorus layer on THE NAMING (visual + score swell; no power change).
- **L10 PASSIVE — HELD THINGS FETCH (per GAMEPLAY_DOCTRINE §3):** once per mission the sprite retrieves one Held Thing that would otherwise need backtracking (a quality-of-life crown).
- **L10 FLAWLESS TRUTH:** a max-level sprite knows you completely — unique per-region idle scene (Cinderwisper sleeps in your flame glow; Pebbleheart lets you carry it like a worry stone). No mechanic. Just love.

**DESIGN LAW: sprites never deal damage and never gate content.** They are companions, not gear — a god for your pocket, not a weapon.

## IV. SKINS — THE MONETIZATION (cosmetic-only, healthy model)
Abilities come from LEVELING; skins change NOTHING but looks (no pay-to-win, ever). Players buy SPRITES, not power.

**Skin families (by rarity):**
1. **FESTIVAL EDITIONS** (common) — festival-garland versions in each region's festival colors; sold at the region's festival season (time-limited rotation = event calendar for free).
2. **MIMIC SKINS** (rare) — the sprite cosplays ITS REGION'S GIANT GOD in miniature: tiny raw-element anatomy echo, miniature element-forged crown-weapon, faction sigil. The Cinderwisp wears the Furnace-King's molten silhouette. Collectors' bait; faction pride purchases.
3. **ARMOR PLATE SKINS** (rare) — the sprite wears its region's mortal armor style in miniature: Forge-Iron cinderwisp, Reliquary-Plate gloamflame. Regional pride purchases.
4. **VERSE SPRITES** (epic) — one per SONG verse collected; visual evolution: the sprite gains a musical note in its element. Purchasable only after the corresponding verse is earned (monetization that RESPECTS the lore — you buy the outfit, you still sing it).
5. **ECHO-INVERSE** (legendary, NOT purchasable) — endgame reward: the sprite rendered in Hollow-inverse (a ember-glow silhouette). The only way to get it is to finish the Empty Pedestal arc with a max-level sprite alive the whole time. Prestige, never money.

**Bundle rule:** skins are per-sprite-type; one purchase = one sprite family skin. Regional bundles (six skins) priced as a set with a small discount. NEVER loot boxes — direct purchase only (community goodwill = long-term revenue).
