# EVERBLOOM — Faction Ability Tree (v1 CANON)

> Unlocked by pledging the flame Mark to THE EVERBLOOM PATRON GOD — THE LIFE MOTHER (Mirielle).
> Resource: FAITH. Free re-pledge. Borrowed bloom — plain kit at rest.

## Bloom Doctrine
Everbloom rites are about **seeds as delayed power**: GROVE SEEDS planted on enemies and ground bloom seconds later into roots, heals, and spores. Patience compounds into a garden that fights for you.

**Passive — Bloom-Kin:** +healing received; seeds you plant bloom 1s faster.

## Tier 1 — The Sowing
1. **SEED-TOUCH** — 5 FAITH. Melee plants a Grove Seed on the enemy (max 5); blooms after 4s into a strangling thorn-burst.
2. **BLOOM WARD** — 8 FAITH. Petal shell for 6s; each hit absorbed heals you 3 HP over time instead.
3. **GROVE'S BLESSING** — Passive. Regenerate slowly near living trees, gardens, and shrine groves.

## Tier 2 — The Growth
4. **BRAMBLE STEP** — 12 FAITH. Dash leaving a bramble trail; enemies crossing are rooted 1.5s.
5. **PETAL PALM** — 15 FAITH. Cone of drifting spores from the open palm — dazed enemies wander and take +25% damage.
6. **SEED MEND** — 10 FAITH. Detonate all seeds in a small radius; heal 4 HP per seed to yourself and nearby allies.

## Tier 3 — The Thicket
7. **THORN EDGE** — 18 FAITH. Weapon crowned with quiet deep moss light for 10s: +30% damage, melee plants 2 seeds.
8. **BLOOM VEIL** — 14 FAITH. Petal-form for 3s — enemies lose you; exiting releases a soothing burst that dazes.
9. **GROVE MARK** — 20 FAITH. Ground rune grows a healing circle for allies; enemies standing in it are rooted every 3s.

## Tier 4 — Capstone
10. **WAKING GARDEN** — 35 FAITH, ultimate, 90s cooldown. 12s as the god's vessel: a living grove erupts around you — allies inside regenerate fast, enemies are slowed and rooted, ALL seeds on the field bloom at once. Ends with a blossom-burst that knocks back.
