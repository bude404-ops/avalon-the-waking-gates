# FACTION-003 CULTURAL DESIGN — LAUREL-SKY (Greek Agon)

Lore is original; the aesthetic is authentic Greek. No historical god names — the titans are ours, the visual language is the Hellenic agon (contest/trial) world: marble arenas, bronze panoply, laurel, verdict-storms.

## Cultural Source
- **Era anchor:** Archaic/Classical Hellas — trial arenas and stadium tiers, marble colonnades, bronze tripods, laurel groves, statue-vaults of fallen champions.
- **Materials:** verdict-bronze (warm, mirror-bright, engraved), storm-glass (clouded Aegean blue glass that hums before thunder), white linen and fine wool (chiton, peplos, himation), olive wood, silver-gilt laurel, aged marble.
- **Motifs:** laurel wreaths (always living leaves, never carved), engraved meander keys, ORIGINAL trial-glyphs (an invented glyph set, never real Greek letters), bronze tripod etchings, olive branches, storm-crowns.
- **Color world:** warm bronze, olive-gold, storm-glass blue, white linen — against living bronze godflesh with olive-gold light seams. (WARM-BRONZE palette between F001's desert gold and F002's cold silver.)
