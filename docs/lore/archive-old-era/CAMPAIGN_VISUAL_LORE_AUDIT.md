# Campaign Visual & Lore Audit — Mythos Gates: Ascension

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Locked:** Aug 31, 2026  
**Purpose:** Confirm each faction's campaign is visually unique and lore-accurate  

---

## Audit Result: ✅ ALL 7 FACTIONS ARE VISUALLY DISTINCT AND LORE-ACCURATE

Each faction's campaign takes place in a different time period, geographic region, and visual environment. Enemies, bosses, and lore themes are all faction-specific.

### No Visual Overlap
- **Empyrean vs Infernal** — both are Mesopotamia, but Empyrean is surface (ziggurats, gardens, light) and Infernal is underground (underworld, darkness, fire). Completely different visual experience.
- **All other factions** — different era, different region, different color palette, different architecture.

## Connecting Lore Campaigns to Gameplay Design

The lore campaigns (docs/lore/18-28) provide the story and visual identity. The campaign gameplay design (docs/CAMPAIGN_GAMEPLAY_DESIGN.md) provides the mechanical structure. Here's how they connect:

| Campaign Gameplay Element | Lore Source |
|--------------------------|-------------|
| Ch1 Tutorial — Nile Delta | Aten Ra Ch1 environment |
| Ch3 Aten Ra unlock — Temple Complex | Aten Ra Ch3 environment + Misweighed Judge boss |
| Ch4 Asgardian unlock — Oathground | Asgardian Ch3 Thingvellir + Forgotten Standard Bearer boss |
| Ch5 Olympian unlock — Citadel | Olympian Ch2 Acropolis + Thunder Tribunal Usurper boss |
| Ch7 Tuatha unlock — Crossings | Tuatha Ch2 Sacred Grove + Crow-Crowned Geas Breaker boss |
| Ch9 Kami unlock — Mirror Road | Kami Ch4 Imperial Shrine + False Reflection Kami boss |
| Ch11 Empyrean unlock — Discord | Empyrean Ch5 Heaven-Engine + Silent Seraph boss |
| Ch14 Infernal unlock — Debt Court | Infernal Ch4 Debt Halls + Volcanic Glass Magistrate boss |
| Ch17 The Last Gate | Multi-faction — uses all 7 faction environments in sequence |

### Faction-Specific Hazards (Tied to Environment)

| Faction | Environmental Hazard | Gameplay Effect |
|---------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Aten Ra | Solar radiation zones | 10 ATK/s to standing deity — teaches positioning |
| Asgardian | Frost patches | Slow movement — teaches repositioning |
| Olympian | Anti-gravity zones | Float up, lose control — teaches ground control |
| Kami | Mirror corridors | Reflections attack — teaches spatial awareness |
| Tuatha | Time-bleed zones | Movement slowed — teaches timing |
| Empyrean | Silence fields | Abilities disabled — teaches basic attack reliance |
| Infernal | Debt zones | HP drain per second — teaches risk management |

These hazards are faction-specific and reinforce the lore theme while providing gameplay variety.
