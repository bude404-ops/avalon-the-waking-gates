# Roots Below the Gate — Campaign Lore

## Canon Function

**Roots Below the Gate** gives the Tuatha worldview its campaign spine: survival through adaptation, memory carried by living terrain, and restoration that is never soft.

The campaign should teach that nature in Mythos Gates: Ascension is not background scenery. It is an extradimensional intelligence with old injuries.

## Narrative Premise

Below a damaged Gate, roots have grown around impossible pressure. They did not invade the ruins. They held them together. Every branch, stone, and moonlit pool stores part of what the Collapse tried to erase.

Vera Root-Memory guides the Creator toward the living terrain, but she refuses easy language. She does not call the land innocent. The land has eaten armies. It has also saved worlds by refusing to let dead roads fully die.

The campaign begins as restoration and turns into a question: when a wounded Realm adapts to catastrophe, does healing mean undoing the scar or learning what the scar now protects?

## Campaign Threat Fit

- **Primary threat:** Beast Realm predators nesting in wounded roots.
- **Secondary threat:** Hollow rot where memory has been drained from living terrain.
- **Environmental identity:** Terrain states that remember the Avatar's actions — the land bears the mark of divine passage.

## Lore-Friendly Dialogue Seed

**Vera Root-Memory:** "Do not ask the root to forgive the axe. Ask whether it still chooses to hold the hill."

**Vera Root-Memory:** "The Grove did not survive because it was gentle. It survived because it learned where to harden."

**Predator Echo:** "Warm law. Soft memory. Breakable paths."

## Tone Guardrails

Avoid cute nature language. Tuatha should feel ancient, patient, and capable of terrible mercy.
