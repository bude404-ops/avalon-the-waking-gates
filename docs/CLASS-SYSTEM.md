# THE SIX CLASSES — CLASS SYSTEM v2 (Sept 8 2026, Big's doctrine: "Update the existing class system using these six core classes")
**Canon supersedes the weapon-class mapping of the Orders as the PLAYER combat system. The Orders survive untouched as the world's pilgrimage institutions (mortal-tier lore, NPC rosters, canon art). THE THREE LAYERS: CLASS = how the player fights (Ancient Mythic Age archetypes) · GOD = who empowers the player (the six Primordial Gods, meaningful play change) · ORDER = who the world's mortals are (Camelot pilgrimage layer, unchanged).**

## THE LAWS
1. One signature weapon per class — the class IS the weapon's way of war.
2. Classes represent HOW YOU FIGHT. The chosen Primordial God represents WHO EMPOWERS YOU.
3. Every class is fully distinct and fully viable solo — no traditional MMO tank/healer/DPS dependency, no forced grouping.
4. Rooted in the ANCIENT MYTHIC AGE — not Camelot, not generic medieval RPG archetypes. Class visual identities render at the MYTHIC tier of the World Visual Distinction Law (pre-Camelot, primordial-adjacent craft), NOT the Arthurian Dark Age mortal tier.
5. The six Primordial Gods each MEANINGFULLY change how each class plays (see the God-Class Matrix).
6. SUPERSESSION — the Warden's signature warhammer ruling supersedes the Arthurian Gear Law's warhammer ban for the PLAYER CLASS KIT. The Gear Law continues to govern mortal-tier world art (Orders, NPCs, factions). Class kits are mythic-age implements: older, stranger, handed down from before the Balancing.

## THE SIX CLASSES (with preserved lineage — strong existing abilities remapped, not discarded)

### SOVEREIGN — SPEAR
**Identity:** Dominion, command, precision, battlefield control. The battlefield bends around the Sovereign's reach and will.
**Lineage:** Inherits the BLADE tree's precision discipline — thrust vectors, guard-break (existing), lock-on combos (existing PilgrimController grammar) re-cut for pole reach.
**Tree (v2):**
- T1 THE THRUST — reach mastery: extended poke range, step-thrust, brace.
- T2 THE LINE — control: sweep-displacement, pin enemies to terrain, line-hold.
- T3 THE STANDARD — command: aura pulses that stagger lesser foes, rally windows, targeted banishment of one enemy's advance.
- T4 CAPSTONE: THE QUIVERING FIELD — the Sovereign plants the spear and for seconds the ground within reach refuses enemies passage (battlefield control as a space claim, not a taunt).
**Visual identity (mythic):** long plain spear with a single carved band; minimal armor, banner-free — command read through posture and reach, not heraldry. **Animations:** pole discipline — wide stance, measured rotations, one-hand plant, two-hand execution.

### RAVAGER — GREATSWORD
**Identity:** Destruction, fury, heavy damage, devastating attacks.
**Lineage:** Inherits the HAMMER tree's heavy-devastation verbs (shockwave slams, poise-crack) re-bladed: fury, not weight.
**Tree (v2):**
- T1 THE CLEAVE — wide arcs, momentum gathering.
- T2 THE FURY — rage-meter windows (existing combo philosophy), unstoppable mid-swing.
- T3 THE RUIN — floor-cracking executioners, stagger capitalization.
- T4 CAPSTONE: THE UNMAKING CRY — a devastation so complete nearby lesser foes are born staggered (the blade's fury as a field effect).
**Visual identity (mythic):** a greatsword too large to be mortal smithwork — plain, edge-true, unadorned. **Animations:** committed momentum arcs, recovery-as-menace, two-handed follow-through.

### WARDEN — WARHAMMER
**Identity:** Protection, defense, counters, barriers, crowd control. THE SIGNATURE WEAPON IS THE WARHAMMER, NOT A SHIELD.
**Lineage:** Inherits the TOWER tree's defense doctrine (existing barrier/intercession abilities) — translated from shield-wall to hammer-law: barriers are GROUND, counters are ANSWERS.
**Tree (v2):**
- T1 THE GRIP — firm stance, impact discipline.
- T2 THE COUNTER — riposte-slam: answer an attacker with hammer authority.
- T3 THE BARRIER — rune-grounded wards: hammer strikes seal circles, chokepoints, retreat lanes (barriers as placed geometry, not a held shield).
- T4 CAPSTONE: THE BULWARK RITE — a sealed ring the Warden enforces: enemies inside are slowed and unable to flee, allies' ground is held (crowd control as claimed and defended terrain).
**Visual identity (mythic):** a long-hafted hammer with a carved head; the read is guardianship in stance and grounding. **Animations:** grounding slams, planted-feet counters, deliberate rotations.

### VEILBORN — TWIN BLADES
**Identity:** Mystery, shadow, deception, curses, mobility, burst attacks.
**Lineage:** Inherits the DAGGER tree whole (twin-blade grammar preserved: reversed-grip work, burst flurries, vanish steps) + adds the shadow-verb layer.
**Tree (v2):**
- T1 THE PAIR — twin-blade flurries, directional dashes.
- T2 THE SHADOW — vanish/blink strikes, feints that break lock-on.
- T3 THE CURSE — mark-bearing cuts: struck foes bleed unluck (weakened, slowed, or briefly confused — the game's curse grammar).
- T4 CAPSTONE: THE MIRROR STEP — a full vanish-reappear behind the struck — the doctrine's deception ceiling (distinct from Hollow mirroring: the Veilborn borrows the trick, never the erasure).
**Visual identity (mythic):** matched plain blades, wrapped grips, half-veiled silhouette. **Animations:** fast committed pairs, recoveries that read as vanish wind-ups, constant repositioning.

### WEAVER — STAFF
**Identity:** Creation, arcane power, constructs, reality manipulation.
**Lineage:** Inherits the SONG tree whole (bell-staff lineage: rite-casting, channeling) — evolved from song to weaving: the staff doesn't play power, it ARRANGES it.
**Tree (v2):**
- T1 THE THREAD — channeled bolts, woven wards.
- T2 THE LOOM — constructs: standing runic forms (ward-sigils, brief servants of shaped light, terrain stitches).
- T3 THE PATTERN — reality verbs: brief slow fields, re-woven ground, unmade traps.
- T4 CAPSTONE: THE SEAM — one local re-write of reality (a few seconds where terrain/rules bend in the Weaver's favor).
**Visual identity (mythic):** a long staff carved with gate-rune geometry, no ornaments beyond the truth of its making. **Animations:** staff-arc channeling, glyph-trails mid-weave, constructs arriving as light before shape.

### WILDBORN — CLAWS
**Identity:** Instinct, nature, hunting, adaptation, speed, primal combat.
**Lineage:** NEW sixth — inherits the world's hunting doctrine (the Forester/ranger instinct lineage from the Order layer) re-cut primal: no bow, no tool — the body IS the weapon. Closest mythic echo: the fauna lords of the bestiary; the Wildborn is the pilgrim who learned war from the wild.
**Tree (v2):**
- T1 THE POUNCE — claw flurries, lunging gap-close.
- T2 THE HUNT — track-read: brief instinct-vision, prey-marking, chase speed.
- T3 THE ADAPTATION — mid-fight shifts (stance-changes that answer what just hit you: gain armor-read after blocking, speed-read after sprinting, senses-read after stalking).
- T4 CAPSTONE: THE BEAST'S REMEMBRANCE — full primal state: a window of animal perfection (speed + instinct-reads + finishers), the wild answering through you.
**Visual identity (mythic):** claw-frames bound to hands and forearms with cord and hide — honest, tooth-and-claw craft. **Animations:** quadruped lunges, low silhouettes, rip-and-recover violence.

## THE NO-TRINITY LAW (locked)
No class is "the tank," "the healer," or "the DPS." Survival is EVERY class's kit: the Sovereign controls space, the Ravager kills fastest, the Warden denies, the Veilborn escapes-and-bursts, the Weaver constructs, the Wildborn adapts. Every tree carries its own defense verb (counter / barrier / vanish / ward / adaptation). Healing belongs to the faith loop and shrines — never to a class role. All six classes clear all content solo; grouping is joy, never dependency.

## THE GOD-CLASS MATRIX (doctrine: gods meaningfully change class play)
The god bond runs on the existing faith loop + Mark-integration law (flame color, rite verbs). Per doctrine, each god must CHANGE HOW THE CLASS PLAYS — not just tint it. Principles + one worked example per god:
- **EMBER (fire god):** verbs of ignition — classes gain burn-capable verbs (Sovereign's sealed ground scorches; Wildborn's pounce leaves embers; Weaver's constructs burn out bright and fast). Play change: aggression windows.
- **TIDE (sea god):** verbs of surge and undertow — displacement and drag (Veilborn's blink becomes a wash-step; Warden's barriers flood-and-hold). Play change: tempo control.
- **STORM (storm god):** verbs of charge and strike-where-you-aren't — chain and teleport-scent verbs (Ravager's arc chains between foes). Play change: extended reach.
- **STONE (stone god):** verbs of mass and stillness — weight added to what the class already does (Sovereign's pinning is heavier; Weaver's constructs last longer, hit harder). Play change: durability trades.
- **BLOOM (bloom god):** verbs of growth and reclaim — living terrain, regrowth (barriers sprout, wounds slow-bleed closed near the marked ground). Play change: attrition advantage.
- **FROST/DEEP (per realm canon):** verbs of slow and deep quiet — enemy deceleration layered on class verbs (curse-cold, hammer-cold, thread-cold). Play change: control trades.
**Rule:** the god changes the class's VERBS and TEMPO, never its identity — a Warden of the Tide still reads as a Warden. Bonded abilities unlock through worship milestones (existing divine-progression doctrine), and the Campaign V god-control finale scales the SAME verbs to primordial size (two-scales law: the god you bonded is the god you will BECOME).

The Arthurian Gear Law continues to govern all mortal world art. The Hollow roster, gods, realms, quests, campaigns: untouched.
