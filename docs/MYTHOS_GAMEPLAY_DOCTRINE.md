# MYTHOS — GAMEPLAY DOCTRINE (Lore-Driven Mechanics)
**Canon v1.0 — Sept 4 2026, BudE404 directive: 'what more can we do on the lore side? If it's good apply it to the gameplay of the game.' Design law: NO MECHANIC WITHOUT LORE, NO LORE WITHOUT A MECHANIC. Every system below cites its buried-truth source. Companion to COMBAT_SPEC + DEEP_LORE.**

## 1. WORSHIP BY DEED — the FAITH economy (source: Faith Loop)
FAITH isn't farmed by grinding — it is WORSHIP, and worship is what mortals SEE you do. Four devotion streams:
| Stream | Earned by | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| PROTECTION | zero-loss escorts/evacuations, defended towns | the steady bank |
| MERCY | releasing Mutefolk (kills release them — the act of ending them GENTLY, not spectacle), sparing broken foes | quiet moments matter |
| SPECTACLE | skill plays — multi-kills, slams, aerial finishers (crowds watch) | the loud engine |
| RITES | lighting braziers, garlanding, festival attendance, repairing niche shrines | the daily devotion |
FAITH caps and giant actives unchanged from COMBAT_SPEC — this doc defines WHERE faith comes from, so playstyle = worship-style. A brutal player and a gentle player both reach the Gate Rite — by different roads (the gods take all worship).

## 2. THE WITNESS — brazier zones (source: Mystery 3, the Sixth Brazier)
Light with a WITNESS cannot be fully erased. MECHANIC: lit-and-watched braziers project a WITNESS ZONE (12 m) in which Hollow units decay 2x and Grimlights cannot spawn. Braziers can be lit (Rite stream) and extinguished by Erased Drakes — relighting is the defense loop of every town siege. THE UNLIT RELIQUARY (endgame key item, one per region's art, findable by attentive players from Act 1) starts unlit and cannot be lit until Aune's rite — carrying it, all Witness zones double.

## 3. THE LENDING — reclamation gameplay (source: Mystery 4, the Echo Paradox)
The Hollow doesn't destroy, it HOLDS. MECHANIC: every Hollow unit killed releases a HELD THING (an item, a name, a memory-fragment) that flows toward the nearest Gate as gray light. REGION RECLAMATION: hollowed land stays visibly gray on the world map until its town's LEDGER-STONE is recovered from a T2 Unmade Champion and re-planted — the region literally re-colors as Hollow drains out. This is the mid-game loop and it makes every kill feel like retrieval, not slaughter. A hollowed NPC can come back. A hollowed REGION can come back. (The horror stays: what the Gate-Worms hold can only be released by killing the Worm — some things wait inside bosses.)

## 4. THE PAUSE — the seventh tension as combat timing (source: Mystery 5 + the recurring motif)
The one-beat silence before every boss phase IS the mechanic: the PAUSE is a perfect PARRY window. Tutorial boss teaches it silently (music drops, parry lands, player learns the beat without a word). Every phase transition in the game uses it. Final boss (the Echo): same beat — but at 33% frenzy the Echo LEARNS to skip the pause, and the player must learn to fight without the tell — until the endgame rite, when the melody the player has collected plays and the Echo's stolen copy stutters, restoring the beat one last time. The seventh tension (SILENCE) is literally the skill ceiling.

## 5. THE SONG — verse collection (source: Mysteries 2 + 7)
Each region has a festival where its verse of the festival song is learned (attendance = Rite faith + a shrine HUM: hum at any shrine for a regional buff, 10 min). Six verses = the full melody. The hidden OTHER VERSE (the lullaby ending) unlocks only after all six + the Archive questline — and singing it at the Empty Pedestal IS the endgame's THE NAMING. The tutorial literally plays the melody in ambient music — the whole game is teaching the final spell to the player's ear.

## 6. THE GRAYING MAP — the strategy layer (source: Hollow = de-coloring; Gates as pressure valves)
World map shows Mythos in living color. Hollow spread grays regions in real time: undefended regions lose color, then their braziers, then names (town names literally fade on the map). Player chooses which region to reinforce between missions — Tower Waves hit the weakest-faith regions (Faith Loop as difficulty director). Saving a region restores color in a visible bloom — the map itself is the scorecard and the emotional payoff. No faction banners needed: COLOR is the faction system.

## 7. NAMING — the anti-Hollow verb (source: Mystery 2 + THE NAMING endgame)
True names destabilize the Hollow. MECHANIC: named T2/T3 enemies (Unmade Champions bear their original armor-name on the Ledger) can be CALLED OUT — a free action that reveals their weak point and staggers them once per fight ("You were Brann's second. Your name is not lost."). MUTEFOLK have no names to call — they can only be RELEASED (mercy). The mechanic teaches the theme kinesthetically: names protect, mercy frees, and the endgame scales both truths to world size.

## 8. DEED MEMORY — the kindness ember (source: item lore: the first-ember shard)
Regional faith decays SLOWLY if the player never performs RITES or MERCY in a region ("the ember dims where no one is kind"). Never punished, never game-over — the Graying Map just creeps. The game never says WHY. Players who read item lore already know.

**SYSTEM SUMMARY — the gameplay IS the theology:** worship feeds the gods (FAITH), light needs witnesses (brazier defense), nothing is truly lost (reclamation), silence is the highest skill (the Pause), the song is the endgame spell (verses), color is the war (the map), names are weapons (calling out), and kindness is maintenance (the ember). Every system is a Mystery made playable.
