# GATE GAMEPLAY LAYER — Built ON the Established Gate Mythology (no lore changes)

Source of truth: docs/lore/MYTHOS_WORLD_LORE.md + BudE404's design principle:

> **"The Gate should feel like a fundamental part of the deity's existence and power, not simply a teleportation portal or fast-travel mechanic."**

Lore anchors this layer uses (unchanged):
1. Mythos forced the deities through the Gates to balance their powers; the Gates are Mythos's own architecture
2. Belief/FAITH is the structural integrity of a Gate
3. The Forgotten One's fractured Gate birthed the Hollow — corruption of Gates is already in the mythology
4. Each faction has one Gate (Ember, Gale, Bloom, Dusk, Deep, Stone)
5. A deity cut off from its Gate is limited; connected, it manifests its true colossal elemental form

**THE LAW THIS LAYER ADDS (mechanics only):**
> **A GATE IS A TETHER, NOT A DOOR.** The deity's power in the mortal realm is capped by the Gate's state. The sprite is what a god looks like through a nearly-closed gate.

---

## 1. GATE STATES (lifecycle — all visible, all mechanical)

| STATE | LOOK | EFFECT ON DEITY | EFFECT ON REALM |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DORMANT** | Grey stone, dead braziers, still air | Only the Sprite aspect can exist; powers capped | Desaturated zone around gate; no elemental weather |
| **ATTUNED** | Sigils flicker, heat shimmer, motes drift out | Sprite gains GLIMPSE powers (a colossal hand, one fire-lance); ascension possible but charge-limited | Palette warms; first motes of elemental weather |
| **ACTIVE** | Full elemental bloom, sky-beam visible across the region | True colossal form can manifest near the gate; full power | Weather turns elemental (ember snowfall at Ember, storm at Gale); biome bloom radiates outward |
| **DESTABILIZED** | Flickering, cracked stone, guttering beam | Colossal form thins mid-battle — forced retreat risk | Weather stutters; motes stop |
| **CORRUPTED** | Inverted palette, purple Hollow veins, whispers | Deity LOCKED OUT of the region — cannot manifest | Hollow spawns THROUGH the gate; realm decays to ash |
| **SEALED** | Inert stone — a monument again | Sprite only, and weakened (no glimpses) | Region goes permanently grey until restored |
| **RESTORED** | New channel-stones, brighter heart-sigil | +Integrity cap, +charge cap — permanent buff | Renewed belief: shrines regen integrity passively |

State transitions are EVENTS — the whole region sees the sky change. When one Gate activates, ALL SIX pulse in sympathy (world broadcast; distant gates on the horizon flash — free navigation and drama).

## 2. THE GATE LINK — the core giant mechanic

The colossal form's power = **f(distance from its Gate)**.
- Inside the **Sanctified Radius**: full power — regeneration, ults, charge efficiency at 100%
- Striding away: the **ESSENCE LINK** bar drains — attacks weaken, abilities cost more, the body visibly THINS (opacity, ember-shed increases)
- At zero link: the form starts failing — forced return or collapse into sprite aspect
- The link bar is a chain sigil on the giant's HUD; the tether can be *seen* — a thin ember thread from the gate to the god when you look back

**Why this is the right mechanic:** it makes giant gameplay ORBIT the gate (battlefield objectives radiate outward from it), creates real positioning tension (deep strikes = risking your divinity), and visually proves the lore: the god's existence in Mythos literally runs through the Gate. No other game's portals do this because for everyone else a portal is travel. Ours is a lifeline.

## 3. PILGRIM-SCALE GAMEPLAY (what the gate does at mortal scale)

- **The sprite IS the limited manifestation** — the lore answer to "why am I small?": a nearly-closed gate can only pass a narrow aspect of a god. (The player's smallness is now canon-deep, not just a mode.)
- **Glimpse powers near an Attuned gate** — 3-second channelings of the true form (colossal hand blocks a collapse; one god-scale fire-lance) — faith-costly, preview-of-ascension moments between full transformations
- **Rites & maintenance** — relight channel-stones, repair sigil-cracks (Discovery-type quests at every gate)
- **Faith banking** — shrines + the gate foot are where kills-banked-FAITH becomes GATE CHARGE
- **Pilgrim roads** — gates are the compass; roads lead to them; travelers on them are story hooks

## 4. FAITH & WORSHIP (the structural-integrity loop, made playable)

Lore says belief is the gate's integrity. Mechanically:
- **Worshipper economy**: rescued civilians → they build shrines → passive integrity regen. Neglect a region → decay. The mortal ecosystem literally holds the Gate together — protection gameplay has structural payoff, not just gratitude
- **Kill-to-faith** feeds GATE CHARGE (the mission-length resource for staying colossal)
- **Miracle quests** at pilgrim scale (save a town) restore BELIEF in a sealed gate's region — belief, not masonry, is what you repair

## 5. GATE ACTIVATION CONDITIONS (rite checklist — all three required)

1. **CHARGE**: banked FAITH above the gate's activation threshold (the gate "remembers" being fed)
2. **RITE**: performed at the foot by the deity's own Warden — the only being who can kindle it (sprite-only sacred act; the giant CAN'T open its own gate — beautiful and correct)
3. **CLEANLINESS**: corruption level below threshold — you cannot kindle a gate while Hollow veins live on it (forces purge objectives before ascension; kills never the goal — cleansing is)

## 6. MISSION OBJECTIVES the Gate generates

ACTIVATE (rite) · DEFEND (structural waves) · PURGE (cleanse veins) · REPAIR (channel-stone collection) · ESCORT PILGRIMS (faith carriers) · STABILIZE (hold through a storm) · SEAL AN ENEMY GATE (the Hollow's fracture-gate — campaign-arc objective) · AWAKEN (a dormant gate as region unlock) · THRESHOLD (walk into the god's realm)

## 7. GATE DEFENSE MISSIONS (giant scale)

- The Hollow attacks the GATE, not just the town: waves target channel-stones and pillars; the giant defends structural integrity with collateral risk (care meter — your slam near your own gate chips it)
- **Mid-fight state change**: if integrity drops below 50%, YOUR OWN colossal form starts thinning (the link flickers) — defense stakes are your own existence
- Success: gate integrity restored + temporary overcharge

## 8. GATE CORRUPTION BY THE HOLLOW

- Corruptor units latch onto gate stone and channel veins outward; purple veins creep toward the heart-sigil — VISIBLE progress bar in world geometry
- Reaching the heart: CORRUPTED state — the gate inverts and becomes the Hollow's spawn door in that region; the deity is locked out
- **Purge gameplay is TWO-SCALE**: the giant defends the gate exterior while the WARDEN enters the gate interior (a Discovery-type inner dungeon) to cleanse the heart-sigil from within — the only scale-combination mission in the game, and only a gate could host it

## 9. GATE-FUSED BOSS ENCOUNTERS

- The Hollow attempts to IMBUE itself via stolen gate-power (the mythology's own trick stolen back) — bosses that grow corrupted colossus forms mid-fight by siphoning a destabilized gate
- Boss phases interact with the gate: the boss corrupts a pillar → your link weakens → restore it mid-duel
- Long-game: the Forgotten One's FRACTURED GATE is the final boss arena — the mythology's origin of the Hollow becomes the endgame door

## 10. GATE TRAVEL — pilgrimage, not teleport (the ruling)

- Lore-safe travel: an ACTIVE gate leads to ITS deity's realm — the realm-threshold (Ember Gate → the Ashfall). You walk THROUGH as a pilgrim, not teleport between mortal points
- Threshold zones are instanced god-territory: earn ascension charges, meet your giant aspect, take relic quests
- **Ruling: NO mortal-to-mortal fast travel through gates.** Mortal travel stays on pilgrim roads (keeps the world big, keeps gates sacred, honors "not a fast-travel mechanic")

## 11. VISUAL & ENVIRONMENTAL LANGUAGE

- Dormant → grey, still; Attuned → flicker + shimmer; Active → full elemental weather bloom + horizon-visible sky-beam + sympathy-pulse across all six gates; Corrupted → inverted palette + veins + whispers
- **Environmental change is permanent and radiates**: an activated gate reclaims its biome (grass through ash, clear water) — the player's deeds visibly mark Mythos
- Scale duality at the same object: the sprite enters the keyhole at the gate's foot; the giant steps THROUGH the 100m frame. One structure, two doors.

## 12. DAMAGE / SEAL / RESTORE

- **Damaged**: charge cap reduced (smaller faith battery), broken channel-stones visible — repair quests
- **Sealed** (by mortal fear, story): inert monument; restoration = BELIEF repair via pilgrim-scale miracles in the region, then a re-rite
- **Restored**: permanent integrity/charge cap buff + threshold zone unlock — restoration is the long-game reward loop
