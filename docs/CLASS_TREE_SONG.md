# CLASS TREE — SONG (Bell-Staff / Cantor) v1 CANON

> Faction-neutral weapon discipline. Class = hands; god = flame. STAMINA/cooldowns, never FAITH.

## Doctrine
The Bell-Staff rings, and the battle obeys. The Cantor is the field's timekeeper: resonance that staggers, silences, and conducts allies. Not a damage class — a rhythm class. Every fight has a beat; the Cantor sets it.

## Tier 1 — The Toll
1. **FIRST TOLL** — Passive. Bell strikes build RESONANCE (stacks to 5); each stack = +4% to all Song actives.
2. **CHIME STRIKE** — 20 stamina. Mid-staff bell hit — small damage, stuns weak enemies 1s, +1 Resonance.
3. **QUIET RHYTHM** — Passive. Resonance decays 50% slower while any ally is in your aura.

## Tier 2 — The Refrain
4. **RINGING CENSURE** — 10s cd. Directed ring — silences enemy casters/interrupters for 2.5s (the anti-caster bell).
5. **PROCESSION** — 25 stamina. A slow wave of golden resonance rolls out: allies hit gain +10% speed 4s, enemies are slowed 15% 4s.
6. **ECHO** — Passive. Every third bell strike repeats at 50% effect without cost.

## Tier 3 — The Anthem
7. **DEEP TOLL** — 12s cd. A single heavy ring at max Resonance: all enemies in melee radius staggered 1.5s.
8. **HARMONY** — 30 stamina. Consume all Resonance: heal allies in aura 3 HP per stack.
9. **STEADY HAND ON THE STAFF** — Passive. Interruptions during rings are 50% less likely to cancel them.

## Tier 4 — Capstone (mastery, not god)
10. **THE GREAT PEAL** — 25s cooldown. The full five-bell peal: for 6s every ring cascades — continuous stagger pulses to enemies, continuous speed/regen pulses to allies. The battlefield conducts to the Cantor.

## Stat pins (engine source of truth)
- Bell base: 8 dmg/strike; Resonance max 5 (+4%/stack).
- Stamina pool 100. Aura radius 8m.
- Synergy: RINGING MARK (Skyrend) and ground runes overlap the resonance grammar; buffs/debuffs use the same modifier system as Gloom/Bloom.
