# CAMELOT CREATURES & MAGIC — the Arthurian flavor pass (Sept 8 2026, Big: 'lets go with your suggestions')

## THE PRINCIPLE
Avalon is the dark mirror of Camelot: the king is dead, the knights are hollow, the light went out. The Arthurian read comes from real romance sources, not generic fantasy.

THE OATHLESS already reads peak Arthurian: a fallen knight-elite, the Knight's sworn brother gone hollow.

## II. THE MAGIC — Merlin's three verbs (not fireballs)
1. WONDER — miracles are rare, quiet, awesome. The Mark rites are renamed ENCHANTMENTS with romance language in all UI + rites.
2. OATH — spoken vows with real power: tribute rites, shrine oaths, and the Knight's 'Sir' is EARNED by an oath sworn at a gate.
3. PROPHECY — the lore layer: the gods dreamed their own deaths; the gates foretell. OPEN CONCEPT (not locked): a Merlin-role lorekeeper NPC who speaks in half-truths.

## III. THE LUMINARY IS OUR GRAIL
The whole game is structurally a Grail quest: a wounded land, a pilgrim carrying the one living light, shrines that heal the world quest by quest. This framing is canon for all lore + quest writing.

## IV. THE GODS (approved template, Sept 8 — example roll art/pending/GOD-STYLE-EXAMPLE-CAMELOT-FIRE.png)
CAMELOT GOES AROUND THE GODS, NOT ON THEM. The mortal world carries the Arthurian read (mail, timber halls, torch processions); gods stay 100% pure element per the full law-stack (Primordial, no-legs torso-up, barely-face, no mouth/hair, primordial hands, two-layer light, goddess feminine-structure). The mortal/god contrast IS the Arthurian collision: Camelot = sworn men and forged steel; the gods = the old pagan wild beneath and before it. The 4 remaining pantheon rolls (Bloomweald pair, Gloambarrow pair) follow this template with their own elements.
