# PROGRESSION & PLAYER-RESPECT LAW (Sept 8, 2026 — Big's doctrine, verbatim canon)

Dictated by BudE404 Sept 8, 2026. This is THE governing doctrine for progression, loot, dungeons, quests, and campaign structure. Where any existing doc (PROGRESSION_SYSTEM_SPEC.md, QUEST_DESIGN_DOCTRINE.md, MYTHOS_CAMPAIGN_BIBLE.md) conflicts with this law, THIS LAW WINS.

## 16. BANNED — THE ANTI-GRIND LIST (all permanently banned from the game)

- Endless randomized gear drops
- Constantly replacing equipment
- Gear score
- Endless stat inflation
- Farming for slightly better versions of the same item
- Mandatory dungeon repetition
- Daily grinding requirements
- Energy systems designed to force repeated play
- Artificial progression walls
- Fear-of-missing-out progression
- "Play every day or fall behind"

**THE LAW:** The player should never feel forced to play constantly just to keep progressing.

## 17. MEANINGFUL PROGRESSION

Progression comes primarily from:

- Story
- Class mastery
- Skill trees
- Divine progression
- Ancient techniques
- Unique relics
- Exploration
- Discoveries
- Quest decisions
- World changes
- Character development
- Cosmetic customization

**A major accomplishment must provide something meaningful and permanent.**

## 18. RELICS

Relics may exist, but they are MEANINGFUL ARTIFACTS, never disposable randomized loot. A relic can:

- Unlock a unique ability
- Change an existing ability
- Reveal history
- Connect to a God
- Alter a quest
- Unlock an area
- Change an interaction
- Provide a meaningful build choice

**Never create hundreds of meaningless statistical items.** (Fits the existing No-Artifact-Mimicry law: the Luminary is the one true relic; relic-tier items are rare, unique, and story-bound.)

## 19. DUNGEONS ARE ADVENTURES, NOT FARMING LOCATIONS

A dungeon feels like discovering a lost piece of the world. It can contain:

- Unique story
- Ancient civilizations
- Hidden history
- Unique enemies
- Puzzles
- Environmental storytelling
- Class discoveries
- God discoveries
- Unique relics
- Ancient techniques
- Boss encounters
- Permanent world changes

**The player never has to run the same dungeon 100 times to continue the campaign.**

## 20. OPTIONAL DUNGEON REPLAYABILITY

Replayability exists but is ALWAYS OPTIONAL:

- Higher difficulties
- Time trials
- Challenge modifiers
- Enemy variations
- Optional bosses
- Mastery challenges
- Achievements
- Cosmetic rewards
- Additional lore

**None of these are ever mandatory for campaign progression.**

## 21. RESPECT THE PLAYER'S TIME

A 20-30 minute session must feel like an accomplishment. A session might involve:

- Completing a quest
- Clearing part of a dungeon
- Unlocking an ability
- Discovering lore
- Advancing a God relationship
- Exploring a new location
- Defeating a meaningful enemy
- Making a story choice

**The player can stop playing without feeling punished for leaving. No daily login pressure.**

## 22. WORLD EXPLORATION — CURIOSITY IS THE REWARD

The world rewards curiosity: buried temples, ancient roads, lost kingdoms, forgotten battlefields, giant remains, hidden caves, sacred groves, ruined cities, sealed chambers, ancient monuments, unknown civilizations.

**Not every discovery gives an item. Sometimes the reward is KNOWLEDGE — and that knowledge can later become important.**

## 23. QUEST SYSTEM — INTERCONNECTED CATEGORIES

- **Kingdom Quests** — politics, succession, oaths, alliances, betrayals, wars, ancient claims
- **Exploration Quests** — ruins, caves, forests, buried cities, temples, lost civilizations
- **Primordial Quests** — reveal pieces of the ancient Gods and their history
- **Class Quests** — explore the philosophy and origin of the player's combat path
- **Divine Quests** — develop the relationship between the player and their chosen God
- **Relic Quests** — recover meaningful ancient artifacts
- **Monster Quests** — investigate unusual creatures and their origins
- **Mystery Quests** — unexplained events whose meaning becomes clearer later
- **Choice Quests** — meaningful decisions with consequences

## 24. CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE — GRADUAL DISCOVERY

**CAMPAIGN I — AGE OF KINGS.** Introduce the Ancient Mythic Age. The player encounters: kingdoms, warriors, monsters, political conflict, ancient ruins, strange legends. The Primordial Gods are mostly distant mysteries.

**CAMPAIGN II — THE FIRST RUIN.** The player discovers something substantially older than the current civilizations. The discovery begins connecting the current world to the Primordial Age.

**CAMPAIGN III — THE AWAKENING.** Ancient structures and creatures begin responding to something. Magic becomes unstable. Old seals weaken. The Gods become increasingly relevant.

**CAMPAIGN IV — THE SIX PATHS.** The player explores the deeper origins of their class and the six primordial philosophies.

**CAMPAIGN V — THE DIVINE CONFLICT.** The player becomes increasingly connected to their chosen Primordial God. Ancient forces begin returning. World-scale encounters become possible.

**CAMPAIGN VI — THE PRIMORDIAL TRUTH.** The player finally discovers the deeper truth behind the Primordial Age and the Fall. The final conflict forces meaningful choices rather than simply "kill the villain." Possible outcomes: Restoration, Destruction, Divine rule, Mortal independence, Rebirth, Another Primordial Age, Sealing the ancient powers, A completely new world order. **The final outcome must be determined by the established lore and player choices.**

## 25. PRIMARY ANTAGONISTIC FORCE

Do not automatically make one of the six Primordial Gods the main villain. Consider whether the existing lore supports an older force associated with the Fall.

**Potential framework: THE UNMAKING** — an ancient force whose objective is not conquest but the undoing of existence itself; its goal is to return reality toward a state before creation. **This concept must be evaluated against existing lore rather than inserted blindly.**

The primary enemy must have: a clear philosophy, a reason for existing, a connection to the ancient world, a relationship to the Fall, a reason to oppose the player, escalating manifestations, major revelations, and a final confrontation appropriate to the mythology.

## 26. ENEMY ESCALATION

Never meet the ultimate threat immediately.

- **Early Game** — monsters, corrupted creatures, human enemies, ancient guardians, local threats
- **Mid Game** — strange creatures, ancient references, broken seals, unexplained phenomena, primordial relics
- **Late Game** — ancient entities, colossal creatures, divine conflicts, primordial manifestations
- **Endgame** — the player realizes the same ancient phenomenon behind the world's greatest catastrophe may be returning

## 27. WORLD BOSSES

World bosses are MYTHOLOGICAL EVENTS, not oversized health bars. They: alter their surroundings, have unique mechanics and multiple phases, tell a story, reveal lore, create consequences, potentially unlock new regions, connect to the Gods, and lead into Primordial Battles when appropriate. **A World Boss victory must permanently matter.**

## 28. PERMANENT WORLD CONSEQUENCES

Major victories change the world: open a new valley, reveal an ancient city, expose buried ruins, change a kingdom's politics, awaken another structure, unlock a questline, reveal Primordial history, strengthen the divine relationship, unlock a divine ability, alter future encounters.

**The player must feel: "The world is different because I did this."**

## 29. THEMATIC QUESTIONS

The campaign explores (never as simple good-vs-evil): What deserves to survive? Is destruction always evil? Should ancient powers return? Can civilization coexist with nature? Should Gods rule mortals? What happens when mortals inherit divine power? Is the ancient world truly dead? What if myths are memories? What does humanity owe its creators? What happens when the old world refuses to remain buried?

## 30. VISUAL DIRECTION

The Ancient Mythic Age should feel: Ancient, Grand, Mythological, Dark when appropriate, Beautiful, Mysterious, Dangerous, Heroic, Otherworldly.

# DOCTRINE PART 2 (Sept 8, 2026 — same transmission, Big's words)

## WORLD VISUAL DISTINCTION LAW

**Avoid making the entire world look like generic medieval Europe.**

The visual design must distinguish:

- **Ancient Mythic civilizations**
- **Primordial ruins**
- **later historical cultures**

**The Primordial Gods must look fundamentally different from ordinary mortals. Their manifestations communicate ENORMOUS POWER and ANCIENT EXISTENCE.**

(Design mapping: mortal culture = the Arthurian Dark Age tech level already locked in the Time-Period Law — that's the "later historical culture" tier. Ancient Mythic civilizations and Primordial ruins each get their OWN distinct visual language, separate from the mortal tier. Gods already carry the Primordial Law — torso-up, barely-face, pure element, zero craft — this law extends that separation to the world itself.)

## 31. CINEMATIC STORYTELLING

Cinematics used STRATEGICALLY. They introduce:

- Major characters
- Primordial revelations
- World-changing events
- God manifestations
- World Boss awakenings
- Major campaign transitions
- Important choices

**Never turn ordinary gameplay into excessive cutscenes. The player stays involved.**

## 32. CORE GAME LOOP

**Explore → Quest → Fight → Discover → Enter Dungeon → Master Class → Develop Divine Connection → Encounter Ancient Threat → Experience Primordial Battle → Change the World → Continue the Campaign**

NOT: Farm → Grind → Replace Gear → Farm Again.

## 33. DESIGN AROUND MEANINGFUL PROGRESSION

Every major system must answer: **"What meaningful thing did the player gain from doing this?"**

The answer must be more substantial than a slightly larger number. The player's power grows because:

- They learned
- They discovered
- They mastered
- They chose
- They earned divine recognition
- They uncovered ancient knowledge
- They changed the world

## 34. FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE — THE TWO SCALES

**The game is an ancient mythological action RPG where the player explores a forgotten world as a powerful hero, develops a unique combat path, forms a relationship with one of six Primordial Gods, uncovers the truth of an ancient catastrophe, and eventually participates in battles so enormous that the player CONTROLS THE GOD ITSELF.**

**CHARACTER SCALE**
- Explore the world
- Fight monsters
- Complete quests
- Master your class
- Discover ancient secrets
- Develop your God connection

**PRIMORDIAL SCALE**
- Face impossible threats
- Channel the God
- Control divine attacks
- Battle colossal beings
- Witness the world being reshaped
- Make decisions that can change the future

**THROUGHOUT THE ENTIRE EXPERIENCE:**
- No mandatory loot treadmill
- No endless grinding
- No requirement to play constantly
- No meaningless gear replacement cycle
- Every major accomplishment moves the player's journey forward

**The player should feel they are progressing through an EPIC MYTH, not maintaining a character on a treadmill.**

(Fits existing canon: the Colossus combat spec + terrain-up god battle form already stage the Primordial Scale; the Pilgrim combat doctrine is the Character Scale. The two-scale structure is the game's final shape.)
