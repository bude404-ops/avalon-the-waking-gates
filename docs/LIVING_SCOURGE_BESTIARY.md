# THE LIVING SCOURGE — Non-Hollow Dungeon Enemies (Canon draft v1, Sept 7 2026)

Big's directive (Sept 7): "We still need to make other enemies that aren't hollow that player will fight in dungeons per regions as well."

## THE LAW OF CONTRAST
Hollows are the absence: colorless, faceless, mournful. THE LIVING keep their color, their faces, and their intent — they CHOOSE to fight you. On screen the read is instant: **color = a thing you can fear or reason with; grey = a thing you mourn.** The Color-Absence Law already guarantees living forms stay colored — this roster makes that contrast EARN its keep.

## THREE LIVING FAMILIES (every realm gets all three)

### 1. REMNANT FOLK — living humans
The ones who stayed when the world dimmed: scavenger clans, fallen orders, debt-cults. They have faces, color, greed, and fear.
- Combat grammar: AMBUSH + FLANK + reinforcement calls; they fight for loot and BREAK AND FLEE when losing (first enemies that want to live — new tool for pacing and mercy mechanics).
- Tone: they are what the pilgrim could become. Never cartoonish evil — desperate, hard-eyed, human.

### 2. FAUNA — living beasts
Each realm's real wildlife, territorial and colored. These are the LIVING TWINS of the realm's shade-beasts — players meet the colored ember-hound first, then later its Hollow echo reads as the colorless version of itself.
- Combat grammar: pack tactics, den defense, retreat-and-regroup; fast, low-poise, swarm.

### 3. CONSTRUCTS — made things
Shrine wards, golems, automata left by the faithful to guard what matters. 100% craft under the Craft-Intact Law — nothing hollow about them, ever. They obey old orders from dead masters.
- Combat grammar: NO stagger, NO fear, slow telegraphed patterns; some are WARD-GATES — dungeon progress fights. Immune to the reliquary soft-CC.
- Tone: they don't hate you. They just won't let you pass. The saddest guard duty in a dead world.

## DUNGEON PACING GRAMMAR (design intent)
- Hollows = the dread pulse (routines, interrupts, the mournful wave).
- Living = the friction (ambushes, fights that think and flee, real menace with faces).
- Constructs = the walls (gates, bosses-lite, pattern fights).
- Standard dungeon template: REMNANT FOLK at the mouth (they got here first) → FAUNA in the wild stretches → HOLLOWED mid-depths (the deep past belongs to the absence) → CONSTRUCT ward-gate before the sanctum → regional elite / dead-god boss.
- Faith economy: living foes drop faith motes as normal; constructs drop TRIBUTE (made things have no faith to release — they pay in offerings). Tuning later.

## ART RULES
- Living enemies: FULL COLOR, real faces, real craft — under no circumstances drained or voided.
- Remnant Folk gear follows each realm's cultural craft (kiln-slag patchwork, storm mail, briar-weave, brazier-brass, salt-rusted iron, strata-stone).
- Constructs render as beloved old machines: worn, repaired, quietly dutiful — never menacing-horror.
- One absence per art piece still applies ONLY to Hollows; the Living have zero absences.
