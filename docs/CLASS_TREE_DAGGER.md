# CLASS TREE — DAGGER (Way-Daggers / Wayfarer) v1 CANON

> Faction-neutral weapon discipline. Class = hands; god = flame. STAMINA/cooldowns, never FAITH.

## Doctrine
Two blades — long in the strong hand (forward grip), short in the off hand (reverse grip). The Wayfarer wins by never being where the blow lands: position, stacks, and exit. Speed is the whole armor.

## Tier 1 — The Path
1. **ROAD LEGS** — Passive. +15% base move speed; dodges cost 20% less stamina.
2. **DOUBLE CUT** — 15 stamina. Forward-grip stab into reverse-grip backcut — two hits in one animation.
3. **READ THE ROAD** — Passive. +10% dodge window against enemies you've struck in the last 5s.

## Tier 2 — The Turn
4. **SLIP STEP** — 8s cd. Sidestep-dash with a brief 0.3s dodge window (the duelist's answer).
5. **SPINNING ANSWER** — 25 stamina. Spin past an enemy; the reverse-grip blade hits as you pass — bonus from behind.
6. **LIGHT PACK** — Passive. All Wayfarer actives refund 5 stamina on hit.

## Tier 3 — The Crossroads
7. **OPENING CUT** — 12s cd. Hit an enemy mid-attack: interrupt + their next attack against you has 25% miss chance.
8. **BOTH EDGES** — 30 stamina. Cross-cut flurry on a staggered enemy: 4 fast hits, finisher applies a wound (bleed 8s).
9. **GONE BEFORE THE DUST** — Passive. After any dodge, +30% speed for 2s.

## Tier 4 — Capstone (mastery, not god)
10. **THE VANISHING ROAD** — 25s cooldown. A blurred five-step weave through up to 4 enemies — each passed through takes a cut, you reappear 8m away unharmed. The Wayfarer's signature exit.

## Stat pins (engine source of truth)
- Dagger base: 9 dmg/hit, 0.5s swing (fastest weapon).
- Stamina pool 100; dodge base 20 stamina; bleed = 2 HP/2s.
- Synergy: faction trail/dash rites (Ashfall Step, Bramble Step, Bolt Step) stack with this tree's mobility grammar.
