# CLASS TREE — HAMMER (Rite Hammer / Smith) v1 CANON

> Faction-neutral weapon discipline. Class = hands; god = flame. STAMINA/cooldowns, never FAITH.

## Doctrine
The Rite Hammer is a promise: everything it hits, breaks. The Smith plays a wind-up game — charge the swing, read the armor, land the one blow that matters. Impact, stagger, and shattered defenses.

## Tier 1 — The Anvil
1. **FORGE DISCIPLINE** — Passive. Holding a full charge no longer drains stamina; +10% hammer damage.
2. **QUICK TAP** — 15 stamina. Fast uncharged poke with the flat — light damage, guaranteed flinch.
3. **SHOCKER** — Passive. Fully-charged hits send a stagger pulse to enemies within 2m of the target.

## Tier 2 — The Tempering
4. **WIND-UP** — 8s cd. Begin charging mid-dodge: the next swing comes out fully charged.
5. **DENT** — 25 stamina. Fully-charged overhead: breaks an enemy's guard/stance (shielded enemies drop their block 3s).
6. **HEAVY HANDS** — Passive. Staggered or downed enemies take +30% hammer damage.

## Tier 3 — The Quench
7. **SPALL** — 12s cd. Charged side-swing that shatters enemy armor plating: -20% to their defense 8s.
8. **FORGE-STEP SLAM** — 30 stamina. Short forward stomp into ground-slam: knockdown wave in a cone.
9. **TEMPERED CORE** — Passive. Below half HP, charge speed +30% (the Smith rages hardest at the anvil's edge).

## Tier 4 — Capstone (mastery, not god)
10. **THE RITE, COMPLETED** — 25s cooldown. The full ceremony: a 2.5s unstoppable wind-up ending in a colossal overhead that deals massive impact damage in a line, shatters all guard, and cracks the ground (terrain hazard 4s). Read it wrong and you eat the counter — read it right and the fight ends.

## Stat pins (engine source of truth)
- Hammer base: 30 dmg full charge (highest per-hit), 1.8s full wind-up.
- Stamina pool 100. Stagger = 1s action lock.
- Synergy: Molten/Crystal/Coral EDGE rites make the charged hit a faction delivery system — the Smith is the best faction-ult setup in the game.
