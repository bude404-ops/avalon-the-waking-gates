# REALM VOICE GRAMMAR — how each realm speaks (dialogue-pass canon)

**Canon:** Sept 6 2026 · The speech-grammar layer for all six realm dialogue passes. Each realm has one sacred grammar: a way its people say true things, lie, and mark importance. The grammar does the worldbuilding — players should tell which realm they're in with the sound off. Promoted from the Ashfall dialogue pass header. Companion doc: ASHFALL_DIALOGUE_PASS.md (the exemplar pass).

---

## ASHFALL (Ashfall) — KILN-SPEECH
Short declaratives. Fire and debt are the two sacred nouns. Oaths must be spoken aloud to be real — a thing unsaid is a thing undone. No "please" — "if you will it." No "thank you" — "the fire remembers." A character lies by NOT LOOKING at the thing.

## SKYREND (Skyrend) — OATH-SPEECH
Promises are measured against weather. Every commitment is spoken with its term ("until the thaw", "for three storms"). Stormmoot creed canon: an oath, once given, belongs to the storm. Breaking speech = a debt of WORDS (they say "I owe you a sentence"). They lie by over-promising — the bigger the storm pledged, the bigger the thing hidden. Farewell: "weather permitting" — and they always permit.

## EVERBLOOM (Everbloom) — GROWTH-SPEECH
Everything spoken is planted — words have seasons, patience is grammar. "Come back when that's taken root." Prune-tenders talk about people as gardens; anger is "blight"; a kindness is "good soil." They lie by CHANGING THE SEASON — "not now, it's winter for that talk."

## DUSKMOURN (Duskmourn) — LANTERN-SPEECH
Quiet, ceremonial, funeral-adjacent. Names are sacred: spoken when alive, KEPT when dead (lantern-honor recitation canon — the Q5 rite is a Duskmourn rite performed in Ashfall). Greetings acknowledge the dead first ("the barrow is calm; and you?"). They lie by SAYING NOTHING — silence is their lie, and it reads as respect.

## MARENTH (Marenth) — LEDGER-SPEECH
Arithmetic as intimacy. Feelings are recorded as debts, tides, and interests ("I'm fond of you; the tide knows what that costs"). Ledger-keeper Nereen (Q4) is the exemplar: pleasant, professional, immovable. They lie by PRECISION — an exactly-true sentence that points the wrong way.

## STONEHEART (Stoneheart) — STRATA-SPEECH
Fewest words in the game. Speech as carving: once said, a thing is PERMANENT — so they say less, slower, and they never repeat. Measured patience is the courtesy; interruptions are "quarry-rudeness." They lie by DEPTH — answering the shallow layer of a question so the deep layer stays buried. When a Stoneheart finally says a full sentence about themselves, the room stops.

## USAGE RULES
1. One grammar per realm, no mixing in a single NPC. (Cross-realm characters — pilgrims, traveling keepers like Nereen — speak their HOME grammar in another realm; the contrast is the characterization.)
2. The lie always follows the realm's lying-rule (not-looking / over-promising / season-shifting / silence / precision / depth). Players learn to hear lies per-region — a learnable skill, never taught in text.
3. The Mark (the pilgrim's own voice) speaks PILGRIM-SPEECH: plain, creed-neutral, deed-words — it belongs to no realm, which is what makes the pilgrim acceptable everywhere.
4. Codex unlock rule holds: no grammar explained in lore text — all learned by hearing.
