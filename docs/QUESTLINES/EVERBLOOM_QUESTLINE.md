# EVERBLOOM — THE HARVEST DEBT (Everbloom chapter questline)

**Written to the Quest Doctrine v1.0. Theology in play (shown, never told):** the harvest debt — every gift is borrowed; something is owed back to the Reach.
**Chapter wound:** the orchard-oath is failing — trees that were promised tenders aren't growing, and the vale's seeds come back hollow.

### Q1 — "THE ORCHARD OATH"
- **HOOK:** A farmer's trees won't take his offerings anymore. He asks the pilgrim to plant one seed for him — just one, to see if it's the trees or the man. *(Want: his grove. Lie: he sold his tender-years to pay a famine debt — the trees know. Cost: if the trees take the stranger's seed, the vale learns what he did.)*
- **TRAIL:** Every tree in the vale wears a debt-token: twine, notches, a child's ring. The forest is a ledger (the world SHOWS the theology).
- **RITE:** Planting the debt-seed with the farmer's own hands guided by the pilgrim's — the planting-oath, half-murmured by the row-folk.
- **CHOICE (two goods):** Plant your own seed (YOUR debt now — Everbloom tracks what the pilgrim owes, all game long) or refuse (his grove dies; the debt stays his).
- **ECHO:** The pilgrim's tree grows in the vale or the farmer's grove stands dead on the hill; row-folk count both.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** Nothing here is a gift. The trees are ledger pages. (Learned by planting in one.)

### Q2 — "THE SEEDKEEP GATE"
- **HOOK:** The seedkeep won't open for the season's distribution. The Keeper's daughter asks the pilgrim to find out why before the vale finds out. *(Want: her father's name intact. Lie: he has been eating the seedkeep's tithes through a lean decade. Cost: exposure ends him; silence starves the next one.)*
- **TRAIL:** The false-bottomed tithe bins; a granary hollowed to its last true measure.
- **RITE:** The season's opening count — the pilgrim reads the true measure aloud (the vale's oldest arithmetic, performed).
- **CHOICE (two goods):** Expose him (thrown out, the daughter inherits a broken vault — and the truth) or cover for him (the lie compounds into next year's famine — the daughter inherits a lie).
- **ECHO:** The seedkeep opens thin or full; the daughter rules it differently either way.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** The harvest debt reaches people, not just trees. (Learned by counting it.)

### Q3 — "THE STILL WATER"
- **HOOK:** A dying dowser asks to see her bravest day in the queen's mirror-water one last time — and asks the pilgrim to hold the staff steady, because her hands can't. *(Want: to die well. Lie: she has never looked — she's afraid the legend isn't true. Cost: the truth, at the end.)*
- **TRAIL:** The hollow under the Still Water — roots like rafters, the mirror-pool that shows each pilgrim the day they were bravest (the Root-Queen's saga-site, walked).
- **RITE:** Holding the staff so the dying can see (the vigil rite; mercy as a physical act).
- **CHOICE (two goods):** Tell her the truth of what the water shows (her bravest day was a small kindness, not the deed she believed — a good death, a broken legend) or let her die believing the legend (a happy death, a lie on the ledger).
- **ECHO:** Her grave-token reads the truth or the legend; the row-folk sing both versions.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** The queen shows truth to anyone — even cowards. Mercy is what you do after. (Learned by holding the staff.)

### Q4 — "THE EVENHAND'S ARBOR"
- **HOOK:** The Bloom Gate opens for every pilgrim equally — always has. But one grove's dead were turned away in the old famine, and their elder wants it said aloud at the arbor. He asks the pilgrim — a stranger with a Mark — to be the one who says it. *(Want: the dead included. Lie: he led the turning-away himself, famine-frightened. Cost: saying it aloud says his part too.)*
- **TRAIL:** The arbor's two seeds — one growing, one sleeping (the Evenhand's riddle, saga-site). The petitioners' path passes both.
- **CHOICE (two goods):** Petition for the dead (the gate groans; BOTH groves pay a season — equality has a price) or for the living (the dead stay outside; the elder's grief rides the pilgrim's ledger home).
- **ECHO:** The arbor's sleeping seed stirs or doesn't; the elder stands inside the gate or at its edge forever.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** Equality is not justice. The Evenhand has been holding that choice for an age. (Learned by paying for it.)

### Q5 — "THE BLOOM GATE" (chapter finale)
- **THE ROLL CALL:** the farmer, the daughter-keeper, the dowser's kin, the elder — whoever the choices left standing.
- **THE RITE:** The pilgrim recites the vale's PLANTED debts to the Gate — the ledger spoken as worship.
- **THE INTERRUPTION:** A **false bloom rises** — an Echo shaped like a garden that hollows instead of feeds (T3). Three phases; final phase drops to pilgrim scale inside the false garden's heart-vine.
- **THE WAKING:** THEVRAINE WAKES THROUGH THE PILGRIM — the gate opens for everyone at once for the first time in an age; the vale's debts bloom into flowers.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** The god's answer to the harvest debt was always going to be: grow anyway. (Learned by watching it.)
