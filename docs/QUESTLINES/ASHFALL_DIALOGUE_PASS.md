# ASHFALL — DIALOGUE PASS v1 (the pilot chapter speaks)

**Canon:** Sept 6 2026 · Gives voice to every named NPC in ASHFALL_QUESTLINE.md (Q1–Q6 + side hooks) per the quest doctrine: want on the surface, lie underneath, cost spoken around — never stated flat. All lines are short, load-bearing, and performable at pilgrim-scale camera. Ashfall speech-grammar per the REALM VOICE GRAMMAR (this doc's header block below — promoted to its own file when the other realms' passes begin).

**Ashfall voice (the grammar this chapter speaks in):** kiln-speech. Short declaratives. Fire and debt as the two sacred nouns. Oaths are spoken aloud to be real — a thing unsaid is a thing undone. Nobody says "please"; they say "if you will it." Nobody says "thank you"; they say "the fire remembers." The lie never comes out in words — it comes out as what a character WON'T look at.

## Q1 — "THE FIRST POUR" · Hearth-Warden Brannas (Forgefall)
*Want: the pour must happen. Lie: she knows the Gate's flame is failing — she sent a stranger so she wouldn't have to watch it be her. She never looks at the Cinder Gate when it's mentioned.*

- **FIRST MEET** (hearth-yards, the cold towers behind her): "You're the pilgrim. Good. My wardens won't carry it — not out of fear. Out of memory. The last warden who carried the seed-flame came back with white in his beard and no words for what he saw. So. A stranger. If you will it."
- **QUEST-GIVE:** "One ladle of the Gate's own fire, carried up to the crucible-core. Speak the pour-oath at the lip and don't spill. That's the whole job, pilgrim. The whole job is everything."
- **MID-QUEST** (seed-flame dims on a silent street — first hint of the siege): "It shouldn't gutter there. That street has a hearth of its own." *(She looks at the street, not the flame. The first crack.)*
- **THE LIE CRACKS** (if the pilgrim asks why a stranger): "Because I light what the Gate gives me. And if the Gate gives nothing this year—" *(stops; looks at her hands)* "—the pour must happen."
- **CHOICE PROMPT** (one seed, two towers): "The keepers' tower or the forges. I won't choose. Whoever darkens, darkens on your will — that's what a pilgrim is FOR. Go on. The city will learn to say your name either way. Or learn not to."
- **COMPLETION:** "The fire remembers you. And whatever else it saw up there — that's between you and the Gate."
- **ECHO BARKS:** keepers' tower dark → "Cold night up on the keep. We say your name warm, anyway." / forges dark → "Guilds are proud. They'll come around. Pride keeps a fire too, in its way."

## Q2 — "THE COLD RELIQUARY" · Mother Brackle (Emberhollow)
*Canon benchmark quest (QUEST_EXAMPLE_EMBERHOLLOW.md). The town elder who relit one lantern against the dark and won't say why she was the only one who knew how.*

- **FIRST MEET** (Emberhollow, one lit lantern in a street of dead ones): "Don't blow it out. Everyone who comes through wants to blow it out or ask about it, and I'm tired of both. You want to know why the hearths are cold and the reliquary's lit? Then do something about the hearths."
- **QUEST-GIVE:** "Something ate our fires. Not stole — ATE. Fires don't go out leaving this little ash. You're Marked. You go down to the vaults and you find what's hungry, and you come tell me. I'll be here. I'm always here."
- **THE RITE (Rekindling, she leads):** "Fingers in the wax like my mother taught and her mother before. Now the oath — after me: *kept light, kept faith, kept flame.* Say it like you're being watched by someone who's deciding whether you mean it. You are."
- **CHOICE PROMPT (evacuate or hold the lanterns):** "I held. Thirty years alone, I held. But I'm old now, and holding is a young town's game. You choose for us — and whatever you pick, I light one lantern for the other road. That's mine. That one I keep."
- **COMPLETION:** "The ledger will say the night Emberhollow rekindled. It won't say who. It doesn't need to. The fire remembers."
- **ROLL-CALL LINE (Q6, at the Gate):** "You see? I TOLD you I'd be here. Now watch an old woman show a god how it's done." *(She relights the reliquary at the Gate's foot.)*

## Q3 — "THE KILN-TENDER'S DEBT" · Kiln-Master Orsa (Kilnreach)
*Want: light the kiln. Lie: she owes the harvest-debt, and the kiln lit means the debt called in. She won't light it herself.*

- **FIRST MEET** (the cold kiln, her hands gray with unused ash): "Everyone asks why I won't light it. Nobody asks why I CAN. There's a difference, pilgrim. Figure out which one you're asking."
- **QUEST-GIVE:** "The oathsmiths' cellar. First-pours that were sworn to Vessamaine and never delivered. Bring them up. What I do with them after — that's kiln-business."
- **THE LIE CRACKS** (cellar returned, oath spoken aloud): "There. Now it's said, and a said thing can't be unsaid. The Kiln-Queen collects oaths like a mother collects chores — she's been waiting for me to light this kiln for nine years. Light it, and she knows I can pay. Leave it cold, and the town freezes honest." *(first direct look at the kiln)* "I never said that last part."
- **CHOICE PROMPT (honor vs mercy on the harvest-debt):** "Honor says everyone pays what they swore. Mercy says the ones who CAN'T pay get to eat this winter. The oath doesn't care what I say at the kiln-lip. So say yours and mean it — Vessamaine hears intent, not words. Lucky for half this town, that's her way."
- **COMPLETION:** "It's lit. The debt's called. Nine years of cold and it comes down to a girl saying the words." *(the fire catches)* "...Thank you. The fire remembers. So will the Kiln-Queen."
- **ECHO BARK:** "Warm in Kilnreach for the first time in nine winters. The old ones say the Kiln-Queen's smiling. The old ones say a lot of things."

## Q4 — "ASH ON THE WATER" · Ledger-Keeper Nereen (Marenth) + Harbormaster Dace (Ashport)
*Nereen's want: the tide-debt recorded as paid. She is pleasant, professional, and utterly immovable — the tide does not negotiate and neither does she. Dace's lie: his grandmother signed the debt away; he's the reason it's unpayable. He wants the pilgrim to "deal with her" so he never has to say what his family did.*

**Ledger-Keeper Nereen:**
- **FIRST MEET** (the docks, book open, waiting): "You're the pilgrim people are whispering about. Good — you'll understand faster than the harbormaster did. This isn't a grievance. It's arithmetic. Ashport's first ships were built on borrowed pearl-lumber. The tide lent. The tide is owed. I'm just the book."
- **QUEST-GIVE:** "The drowned ledger's in the old-water vault — the original signatures, the original shame. Read it before you decide anything. The sea keeps everything, pilgrim. EVERYTHING. That's not a threat. It's a filing system."
- **TRAIL BEAT** (the vault, other realms' pages): "You see them? Every realm keeps pages in every other realm's book. The gods knew what your merchants pretend not to: nobody stands alone. Not ports. Not pantheons."
- **CHOICE PROMPT:** "Pay, and the fishers ground a season and the harbor's blessing stands. Refuse, and the fleet sails fat and my book records your will — and my word travels ahead of you. I go to Marenth next month. I'm ALWAYS in Marenth next month."
- **ECHO BARKS:** paid → "The tide is square with Ashport. I don't get to say that often. Enjoy it." / refused → "The book says what it says. The interest says it louder." *(she travels to Marenth; her greeting there is warm or cold per the player's choice)*

**Harbormaster Dace:**
- **FIRST MEET:** "You see her? Standing on MY docks with her book? Deal with her, pilgrim. Whatever she wants, the harbor can't spare it, but deal with her."
- **THE LIE CRACKS** (after the vault — if confronted): "My grandmother's name is on that page, isn't it." *(long pause)* "She signed it away for the lumber. Said the tide would outlive the debt. Well. The tide outlives everything, Dace. Ask her — she never says it, but I hear it every night."
- **ECHO BARKS:** paid → "The fleet grounds. The blessing stands. We'll salt the fish we didn't catch and tell your story by winter." / refused → "Fat holds, full nets. Don't look at me like that — you chose. Now Nereen's ledger carries ME, and I sleep fine." *(he doesn't.)*

## Q5 — "THE SILENCE'S MOUTH" · Vigil Corm (the charcoal forest crossroad)
*Want: rest. Lie: the silence took his voice mid-hymn; he guards what he can no longer name. His voice is cracked — half of his lines trail into hymn-cadence and stop. He is the chapter's saddest mouth and its best one.*

- **FIRST MEET** (the crossroad, a month of standing in his eyes): "You can hear me. You're the first thing that— no. First things first. I want sleep, pilgrim. I've watched this road a month and NOTHING crosses. Relieve me."
- **QUEST-GIVE:** "The milestone. Watch it. No — watch it the way you'd watch a mouth you suspected of breathing. My order taught: whatever you cannot name, hold the line until the Light names it for you. Something here stopped letting us name things."
- **MID-QUEST** (the fracture shaped like a throat): "There. THERE. You see the shape it makes? That's a throat, pilgrim. I've been standing on a throat and I— *(his voice cuts mid-word; he covers it, waves: continue)* —and I couldn't say so. It eats the saying."
- **THE LIE CRACKS:** "I stood the watch because the silence took my voice mid-hymn and I was afraid that if I stopped standing—" *(cracks; he laughs, one dry note)* "—well. A Vigil who can't sing hymns is just a man with a spear. So I stayed a spear."
- **RITE (the pilgrim speaks the dead names; Corm mouths along — voiceless):** "*Kept light. Kept faith. Kept flame.*" *(mouthing; then, cracked but THERE:)* "Kept... name. That one's mine. That one I get back."
- **CHOICE PROMPT (seal now vs bank for the Rite):** "Seal it, the forest sings at dawn. Bank it, the forest holds its breath a while longer and your Rite burns brighter for it. I held my breath for a month, pilgrim. It's not nothing. But it's not everything either." *(the only NPC who names both goods kindly — his chapter function: absolution either way)*
- **ECHO BARKS:** sealed → "Dawn came with birdsong. BIRDSONG. I wept like a founding novice." / banked → "Still quiet out there. But quiet's not gone anymore. Quiet with a deadline. We can work with that."
- **ROLL-CALL LINE (Q6):** "Where the pilgrim stands, the Vigil stands. New voice. Same watch."

## Q6 — "THE FURNACE-KING'S VIGIL" · The Mark (the pilgrim's own, at the Cinder Gate)
*The Mark speaks the town's deed-ledger back to the Gate — the player re-reads their own story to the god. The Mark's voice is the pilgrim's own, one register deeper.*

- **RITE OPENING:** "Ashfall. Hear your ledger." *(the choices read back — tower lit or dark, debt paid or owed, forest sealed or hoarse — in the pilgrim's own deed-words from the chapter)*
- **THE INTERRUPTION** (the caldera splits; the Echo of the Forgotten rises): "That is not your king. That is the SHAPE of worship with the worship burned out. Hold the ground. The god-light holds the shell. The heart is yours — CLIMB."
- **THE WAKING** (the ash falls upward; Vaelthorn rises through the completed rite): "...Ashfall. Your god was never far. He was under everything the whole time. **Rise and receive your fire.**"

## DESIGN NOTES (for the Unity quest system)
- Every quest-giver has: FIRST MEET / QUEST-GIVE / LIE-CRACK / CHOICE-PROMPT / COMPLETION / 2 ECHO BARKS. Six beats, all pilgrim-camera performable. ~10–16 lines per NPC, whole cast ≈ 90 lines for the chapter.
- The lie-crack is always triggered by player action (vault read, cellar returned, fracture seen) — never by a quest stage alone.
- Echo barks are conditional on the player's recorded choices (the town ledger entity) — the same bark system feeds the Q6 roll call.
- Corm's voice-crack is a text/audio effect: lines end at em-dashes. In audio, mid-line dropouts (the silence eating the word).
- The Mark's rite lines are generated from the player's actual deed-log — the ECHO beat made mechanical.
