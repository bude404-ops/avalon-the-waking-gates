# DUSKMOURN — THE RELIQUARY LEDGER (Duskmourn chapter questline)

**Written to the Quest Doctrine v1.0. Theology in play (shown, never told):** the reliquary-honor — the dead are owed light, and carrying it is the price of passage.
**Chapter wound:** a town's dead have stopped accepting light — braziers gutter when carried to graves, and the unlit rows spread.

### Q1 — "THE NICHE"
- **HOOK:** A gravedigger can't light his mother's brazier-niche. He asks the pilgrim to carry the light instead — his hands shake too badly. *(Want: her niche lit. Lie: he never told her something before she died, and he's afraid it's the reason. Cost: the unspoken thing is a ledger-forgery — hers, done for him.)*
- **TRAIL:** The light dims only on HIS mother's row — the trail is one row of graves and what the dimness means.
- **RITE:** Speaking the unspoken at the niche — the vigil words the gravedigger can't say, carried by the pilgrim (the dead hear; the light steadies as the truth lands).
- **CHOICE (two goods):** The truth is a debt-forgery — confess it at the ledger-house (her name is corrected, his inheritance of shame is public) or carry the correction quietly (her niche stays lit, the ledger stays wrong, one clerk knows).
- **ECHO:** The row stays lit; the ledger-house treats the pilgrim as a witness of record either way.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** The dead hear. Light is a conversation. (Learned by being its interpreter.)

### Q2 — "THE CHOIR IN REVERSE"
- **HOOK:** A town can't mourn — a Still Choir (T2 canon) hums their unspoken griefs backwards in the barrow-field. A Vigil asks the pilgrim to stand the night watch with her. *(Want: her town mourning again. Lie: half the hummed griefs are hers. Cost: the vigil is her penance, and she's asking for company.)*
- **TRAIL:** The hummed fragments map to every unsaid goodbye in town (the Hollow wears what people swallow — witnessed, not explained).
- **RITE:** THE VIGIL — silence, held torches, until dawn (Duskmourn's protection is paid in attention).
- **CHOICE (two goods):** Break the vigil to save one wanderer walking into the field (the Choir escapes; the town mourns freely but one niche goes dark tonight) or HOLD (the Choir dissolves at dawn; the wanderer is lost; the town grieves whole).
- **ECHO:** The barrow-field sings at dawn or one grave stands open; the Vigil stands with the pilgrim from here on (roll-call entry).
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** Protection here is attention. The Hollow harvests the unsaid. (Learned by holding a torch all night.)

### Q3 — "THE LEDGER OF THE UNLAMENTED"
- **HOOK:** The Barrow-Queen's ledger has a page that won't close — a village that died un-mourned, and the Hollow now wears their shapes. The page-keeper asks the pilgrim to recover the village's one surviving name-token. *(Want: the page closed. Lie: he was the one survivor who didn't go back. Cost: closing the page means reciting names he's spent his life not saying.)*
- **TRAIL:** The drowned village under the fen-ice — hollowed villagers walking their old routines (MUTEFOLK, T1 canon); the token in the chapel eaves.
- **RITE:** THE RECITATION — the recovered names spoken into the ledger (the queen's own rite; the reason she never speaks is that the recitation never ends — performed, not told).
- **CHOICE (two goods):** Close the page (the Hollow loses the village's shapes — the fen is safer, the survivor loses the last of them) or leave it open (the shapes still walk, but one hollowed mother-shape can still be found and released gently by name, someday).
- **ECHO:** The fen-ice is quiet or it breathes; the page-keeper's hand rests or doesn't.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** SENNETH never speaks because the recitation never ends. (Learned by doing a page of it.)

### Q4 — "THE LAST WATCH"
- **HOOK:** At dusk, the realm's brazier-niches brighten as something passes — the Bone King's patrol. A flame-child asks the pilgrim to walk one street of it with him, to see if the brightening still works. *(Want: proof the king still walks. Lie: the child's own street stays dark — always has. Cost: finding out why costs the town its founding story.)*
- **TRAIL:** THE DUSK-WALK — keeping pace with a god's patrol without being noticed by it (stealth-pilgrimage; the niches brighten and dim in waves around the pilgrim — the world SHOWS the saga).
- **RITE:** The walk itself; at the dark street, the vigil-halt — torches up, silence held at the boundary.
- **CHOICE (two goods):** The king halts at the dark street too — a hidden wrong from the town's founding (a murder turned out, generations back, and built over): report what the halt means (the town falls apart, the light stands; truth gets a grave) or let it stay dark (the town endures, built on a lie the god also watches).
- **ECHO:** The child's street lights at dusk or never does; the town either rebuilds or keeps its foundation-story.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** MORVAINE walks the town like a father checking the sleeping house. (Learned by keeping his pace.)

### Q5 — "THE BARROW GATE" (chapter finale)
- **THE ROLL CALL:** the gravedigger, the Vigil, the page-keeper, the reliquary-child — every light the pilgrim carried stands at the Gate, held.
- **THE RITE:** The pilgrim recites the chapter's CORRECTED LEDGER — every name the player's choices put right — back to the Gate.
- **THE INTERRUPTION:** A **false reliquary rises** — an Echo shaped like light that hollows what it touches (T3). Three phases; final phase drops to pilgrim scale inside the false light.
- **THE WAKING:** MORVAINE WAKES THROUGH THE PILGRIM — the barrow-downs light like a circuit at once, and the unlit rows... the choice from Q4 stands in the brightness.
- **TRUTH-SLIVER:** The realm of owed light was always one long kindness. (Learned by owing it.)
