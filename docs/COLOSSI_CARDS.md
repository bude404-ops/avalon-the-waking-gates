# THE TWELVE — King and Queen Colossus Cards
Canon: v9 PRIMORDIAL 12-SET (kept Sept 5, 2026). Each colossus has its own art file (art/approved/*-EMERGENCE-CANON.png) and its own card below. The god that rises at the Gate Rite matches the pilgrim's aspect — a male pilgrim wakes the King, a female pilgrim wakes the Queen. Both aspects are canon per faction.

*Name sweep applied per the AVALON NAME BIBLE (canon v1.1): gods carry their Sleeping Monarch names; realms and Gates use the locked Avalon canon. Art files now carry the canon god names too (art/approved/<GOD>-<TITLE>-EMERGENCE-CANON.png).*

## VAELTHORN, the Sleeping King of the Ashfall — the Furnace-King
**Faction:** Ashfall · **Realm:** The Ashfall · **Gate:** Ember Gate
The first of the Twelve to be dreamed of again. Vaelthorn is the mountain that remembered it was a king — eon-layered basalt and shrine-crowned in obsidian monoliths, risen over the Ember Gate like a father standing over a hearth no one else could keep lit. Pilgrim offerings fused into his strata tell of centuries of unanswered prayers.
*Rite line: When the lantern lights where he dreams, the Ember Gate opens, and the mere burns warm again.*

## VESSAMAINE, the Sleeping Queen of the Ashfall — the Kiln-Queen
**Faction:** Ashfall · **Realm:** The Ashfall · **Gate:** Ember Gate
Vaelthorn's other aspect. Where the King is the hearth, the Queen is the kiln — the shaping fire, patient and total. Her magma pour and ember-flower gardens mark where the mere's forges once stood, and her shrine-crowned brow still carries the braziers of the first offering-lights.
*Rite line: She wakes for the pilgrim who mends what fire has broken.*

## HAELDOR, the Sleeping King of the Skyrend — the Storm-King
**Faction:** Skyrend · **Realm:** The Skyrend · **Gate:** Gale Gate
A cliff that learned to thunder. Haeldor's strata buttresses carry wind-shrines and leaning watch-towers, and his storm-bell shrine-crown still rings when the Hollow climb the ridges. He is the wall the storm broke against for a thousand unanswered winters.
*Rite line: When he rises, the bells answer for themselves.*

## SYLWENNA, the Sleeping Queen of the Skyrend — the Gale-Queen
**Faction:** Skyrend · **Realm:** The Skyrend · **Gate:** Gale Gate
The storm's mercy, in the aspect that wakes for female pilgrims. Sylwenna's streaming squall-crown and slate strata read as the eye of the weather — the one still place in the storm. Sky-burial platforms grown on her shoulders held the March's dead with honor.
*Rite line: The storm is not the enemy. It is a queen that forgot who she guards.*

## THEVRAINE, the Sleeping King of the Everbloom — the Bloom-King
**Faction:** Everbloom · **Realm:** The Everbloom · **Gate:** Bloom Gate
A forest that decided to stand. Thevraine's bark-strata body, heartwood ridge, and blossom-bough crown carry hanging gardens and overgrown shrines — a whole sacred grove grown into a king. His sap-gold light is the Weald's spring, long overdue.
*Rite line: What grows back after him is worth every winter.*

## MIRIELLE, the Sleeping Queen of the Everbloom — the Root-Queen
**Faction:** Everbloom · **Realm:** The Everbloom · **Gate:** Bloom Gate
The Weald's other answer. Mirielle's flowering-vine cascade and petal-shed are the Everbloom's own blossom returned as a guardian — the aspect that wakes for those who plant rather than take. Her bark-cliff chest holds grove-shrines older than any road.
*Rite line: She rises where something was planted and kept.*

## MORVAINE, the Sleeping King of the Duskmourn — the Dusk-King
**Faction:** Duskmourn · **Realm:** The Duskmourn · **Gate:** Barrow Gate
A barrow that refused to stay buried. Morvaine's bone-pale chalk strata, shadow-ash plume, and barrow lantern-crown keep the moor's dead lit against the Hollow — the aspect of dusk that protects rather than mourns. Candle-shrines on his shoulders never went out, even when the prayers stopped.
*Rite line: The lanterns on his crown are every grave-light he saved.*

## SENNETH, the Sleeping Queen of the Duskmourn — the Barrow-Queen
**Faction:** Duskmourn · **Realm:** The Duskmourn · **Gate:** Barrow Gate
Dusk's keeper-aspect. Senneth's shadow-mist veil and ivory ridge read as the Duskmourn's night itself standing guard — the quiet before the Hollow come, holding the Barrow Gate like a wick holds its flame. Her wisp-light is cold and it is kind.
*Rite line: She is the dark that does not swallow.*

## NEROVANE, the Sleeping King of the Marenth — the Tide-King
**Faction:** Marenth · **Realm:** The Marenth · **Gate:** Deep Gate
The sea's standing answer. Nerovane's sea-stack arms, lighthouse shoulder, and sunken sea-shrine crown are every harbor's lullaby made colossal — risen in the shallows over the half-sunken Deep Gate, kelp-draped, still keeping the tide's promise.
*Rite line: The tide always came back. So does he.*

## YLSANNE, the Sleeping Queen of the Marenth — the Deep-Queen
**Faction:** Marenth · **Realm:** The Marenth · **Gate:** Deep Gate
The sea-foam aspect that wakes for female pilgrims. Ylsanne's foam-and-kelp crest, coral standing-stone crown, and pearl-green tide-light are the Vale's memory of being beautiful before the Hollow came. Offering-floats still fused in her strata carry the harbor's old wishes.
*Rite line: She rises like the sea forgives — all at once.*

## GRATHWYN, the Sleeping King of the Stoneheart — the Stone-King
**Faction:** Stoneheart · **Realm:** The Stoneheart · **Gate:** Rune Gate
The mountain's oldest name. Grathwyn's granite strata, crystal-spired crown-growth, and true trilithon shrine-crown — a ring of standing stones with lintels, the old henge grown across a god's brow — carry cairn-fields and mine-shrine ruins from the Fell's first century.
*Rite line: The henge was never a door. It was a crown waiting for its king.*

## BERGRUNE, the Sleeping Queen of the Stoneheart — the Rune-Queen
**Faction:** Stoneheart · **Realm:** The Stoneheart · **Gate:** Rune Gate
The bedrock aspect. Bergrune's dust-mote plume, geode-lit seams, and henge-crown are the Stoneheart's patience given a shape — the aspect that wakes for those who carry weight without complaint. Her watch-tower shoulder still holds the mountain's watch.
*Rite line: Everything built on her holds.*
