# LIVING-CAMPAIGN-BIBLE — AVALON's three pillars
(Bude's Sept 14 2026 directive: "the freedom of DnD and depth of quests and such and
perhaps they can always be evolving after the main storyline? but combat like Diablo with
the dungeon smashing and exploration of running around")

## PILLAR 1 — D&D FREEDOM (the world says yes)
- The Six-Campaign Arc is the SPINE, not a corridor. Quests can be approached out of
  order, skipped, or solved differently; the world reacts to what the player DID.
- Knowledge = unlock stays the master law (fast travel, routes, lore): the map fills
  in because you WALKED it, same as a DM's revealed fog of war.
- Choice with consequence: quest forks that close doors elsewhere — D&D's "the DM
  remembers." NPCs reference your past deeds by event, not generic flag text.
- Exploration is the reward loop of the overworld: shrines, waystations, graves, and
  mythic-pocket GATES are found by running the world — never menu-selected.

## PILLAR 2 — THE EVOLVING STORY (the campaign never ends)
- Main arc: authored, one-time, mythic pockets as locked by WORLD-AND-DUNGEON-LAW.
- AFTER the main storyline the world KEEPS PLAYING (D&D campaigns live past level cap):
  a) EVOLVING QUESTLINES — NPC threads that continue, branch, and conclude in chapters
     released over time; old characters return changed by your shared history.
  b) WORLD-BOSS PULLS — realm titans stir on rotation; a titan's waking is a realm
     EVENT (weather shifts, ember-fall, creature migration) before it's a fight.
  c) REALM SEASONS — the realms shift state (flooded Marenth, ember-wane in Ashfall),
     re-seeding quests, routes, and hazards. The world you knew last month is not the
     world this month.
- Post-story content is authored + curated (anti-grind stays: no filler quests, no
  repeat-text boards — evolution, not treadmill).

## PILLAR 3 — DIABLO COMBAT FEEL (smash, loot, run)
- MOMENT-TO-MOMENT: dense packs, readable elites with affix tells, hit-stop + stagger +
  ember-dim feedback (the locked no-numbers HUD law holds — damage is FELT, printed
  nowhere), magnet strikes and input buffering per the Stage B control bible.
- THE SMASH LOOP: mythic pockets = the Diablo runs — clear rooms, chain kills, break
  the pocket's keystone, claim the vault. Pockets stay one-time in the main arc.
- ENDGAME POCKETS: after the main story, SHIFTING GATES open — re-folded pockets
  (new layouts, new elites, new hazards under the same realm design language) so the
  dungeon-smash loop is endless WITHOUT recycling authored story content. Rift law:
  shifted pockets are explicitly realm-warped — never "the same dungeon again."
- LOOT (Diablo's engine, AVALON's soul): RELICS — gear that carries myth, not stat
  spaghetti. Lineage upgrades for the class kit; relics as earned artifacts with names,
  deeds, and one live power read (ember-glint on drop; power felt in play, never a
  spreadsheet). Loot chase = relic hunting in endgame pockets + titan vaults.
- BUILD DEPTH: class kits + weapon lineages + relic passives give the Diablo build
  variety WITHOUT a stat wall; the phone stays one-hand friendly.

## PRODUCTION ANCHOR (how this ships)
Pillar 3's feel layer rides the EXISTING build order: Stage A feel test -> Stage B
combat layer (attack/dodge/lock-on cadence = the moment Diablo feel is judged) ->
horde density + elites -> endgame Shifting Gates after the arc. Pillars 1-2 are content
+ systems law: the overworld quest spine ships with the realms; evolving questlines and
realm seasons are post-arc updates BY DESIGN (that's the point of them).

## LAW NOTES
- Supersedes the "campaign ends" read of the Six-Campaign Arc: the ARC ends, the WORLD
  doesn't.
- Anti-grind law refined, not broken: authored pockets stay one-time; the endless loop
  is explicitly realm-warped endgame content + world events.
- No damage numbers, no quest-marker vomit, no loot beams — muted grimdark holds;
  discovery stays earned.
