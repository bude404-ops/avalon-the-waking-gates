# THE SEAMLESS WORLD & MYTHIC POCKET LAW

Big's ruling, Sept 10 2026 — "Yes codify that i like that." Governs how the player moves through the world, how dungeons exist inside it, and how travel unlocks. Companion to `PROGRESSION-AND-PLAYER-RESPECT-LAW.md`, Mythic World Law, and `AV-UI-BIBLE.md`.

## LAW 1 — THE SEAMLESS MYTHIC OVERWORLD
The overworld is a **continuous mythic landscape, one per realm** — no menu-select levels, no level-select screens. The player RUNS the world.

- Every realm renders at god-scale staging per Mythic World Law: standing-stone avenues, megalithic terraces, titan faces carved in living rock. Never simple villages.
- Quests **pull** the player along mythic routes — shrine paths, old roads, standing-stone avenues — instead of checklist map pins. Direction and curiosity, not task lists.
- Crossing into a new realm fires the **REGION TITLE CARD** (Tsushima law, AV-UI-BIBLE): realm name in serif caps, one-line epigraph, region accent underline, ~2.5s fade. This is the signature traversal moment.

## LAW 2 — INSTANCED MYTHIC POCKETS
Dungeons are **NOT physically buried in the overworld terrain**. Each dungeon is an **instanced mythic pocket entered through a landmark** — a broken archway, a barrow door, a sealed gate, a hollow beneath standing stones.

**Gameplay law:**
- A dungeon may be **colossal inside while the entrance reads small**. Primordial spaces don't obey mortal geometry — cathedral-scale interiors are canon, and they never need to "fit" the overworld.
- Every dungeon is a **one-time authored adventure** (anti-grind law holds): bespoke layout, bespoke encounter, bespoke reward. Replay is optional, never required.

**Visual law:**
- Each dungeon interior gets **its own full art direction and dramatic lighting** — cold two-layer light, the realm's aura colorway, and for Hollow sites the dead-lantern darkness (Lantern Law).
- The overworld/dungeon **contrast is the visual payoff**: bright mythic overworld -> step through the gate -> the dark. The threshold moment is a designed beat, not a loading screen to hide.

**Technical law (mobile-first):**
- Instancing keeps phones from melting: the overworld streams light; dungeon pockets load bespoke scenes. Mobile-first law (AV-UI-BIBLE LAW 0) is served by the split.

## LAW 3 — DISCOVERY TRAVEL
- Travel starts **on foot**. Exploration is rewarded with knowledge first (anti-grind law).
- **Shrine-gates the player has DISCOVERED become fast-travel points.** Knowledge is the unlock — nothing bought, nothing farmed.
- Discovery-travel feeds the **world-boss system**: mythic events pull the player across realms, and the shrine network makes answering that pull a journey, not a teleport-spam.
