# MYTHOS GATES: ASCENSION — MASTER GAMEPLAY ARCHITECTURE (v1.0)
## The canonical unification of all design layers. Supersedes nothing — commands all of them.
Sources: MYG_MAP_MISSION_ARCHITECTURE · COLD_RELIQUARY_MAP_ARCHITECTURE · GATE_MECHANICS_GAMEPLAY_LAYER · CORE_GAMEPLAY_STRUCTURE. Lore is untouchable and untouched.

## THE ONE QUESTION

**"What can we do with the difference between being a tiny mortal-scale Sprite and a primordial Giant God that another fantasy RPG cannot do?"**

The five answers (established, non-negotiable — every system below must serve at least one):
1. **Reconnaissance payoff** — sprite knowledge becomes giant intel
2. **Emotional inversion** — the horror you fled becomes the pest you crush
3. **Landmark memory** — places lived-in become held-in-hand
4. **Protection inversion** — the world that sheltered you needs you as its shelter
5. **Physical continuity** — one geometry, two games

Plus the master line, the emotional identity of the product:
> *"I've been here before — but now I'm seeing it from the perspective of a God."*
Every region must be built to produce that sentence at least once.

---

## THE UNIFYING SPINE — why Sprite + Giant is ONE game, not two

Five shared systems bind the scales together. If any of the five breaks, the game splits into two disconnected modes. These are the load-bearing walls:

1. **ONE GEOMETRY.** Every giant battlefield is walked-first sprite geography (min 3 dual-meaning landmarks — Map Architecture law). No disconnected arenas. Ever.
2. **ONE ECONOMY.** FAITH flows through everything: parries and kills bank it, glimpses/manifestations/ascensions spend it, gate integrity is stored in it. The player never holds two currencies of power.
3. **ONE TETHER.** The Gate layer rules all manifestation: glimpse = nearly-closed gate, manifestation = rite-stone crack inside gate radius, ascension = active gate. Why you are small, why you grow, why you fade — one canon answer.
4. **ONE INPUT LANGUAGE.** Tap-to-move at both scales; the same thumb drives both bodies. Precision verbs differ (sprite: slash/roll/parry — giant: sweep/slam/position) but the hands never relearn the game.
5. **ONE LEDGER — THE WORLD REMEMBERS.** Every destruction, rescue, and rite writes to persistent world-state (the Consequence Ledger below). The giant's deeds change what the sprite explores; the sprite's discoveries change what the giant can do. The two scales are in dialogue through the world itself.

**The design test for any proposed feature:** "Does this deepen the scale dialogue, or does it serve only one side?" Features that serve one side only get cut or paired.

## ANALYSIS — weaknesses in the concept, named honestly

**W1 — The Duality Tax.** Two cameras, two combat kits, two pacing rules — the hardest engineering and balancing problem in the project. *Mitigation:* the five shared systems above; beat discipline (3–6 min); every asset double-duty. *Rule:* we never add a third control context (no mount systems, no vehicle lanes) — two is the permanent max.

**W2 — Mode sprawl at the endgame.** Six post-campaign modes as listed is a content famine on our scale. But they're really THREE archetypes wearing six coats (see Endgame). Building six would produce six shallow modes; collapsing to three pillars produces three deep ones.

**W3 — Tower mode, as described, is the most generic thing in the brief.** Every ARPG has a wave tower — it reinforces nothing about our identity. *Fix: the tower IS the Gate.* THE THRESHOLD TRIALS: you ascend THROUGH the gate's interior — floors are gate-layers (mortal stone → divine alloy → the seam of the fracture), sprite floors test precision, manifestation floors test spatial control, the top floor is a colossus. Structure per your spec (sprite waves → elite → manifestation → colossal → boss) but it lives INSIDE the mythology, wearing gate anatomy. Same content, total identity reinforcement.

**W4 — Destruction without consequence is an expensive gimmick.** Randomly breakable worlds eat mobile budgets and produce "why did that matter?" *Fix: the CONSEQUENCE LEDGER.* All destruction is authored (scripted set-pieces + authored destructibles — never systemic city-ruin), and every destructible writes ONE of two entries:
   → **BOON:** a new passage, loot, lore, or lane for one of the scales
   → **WOUND:** a new Hollow entry point, a damaged friendly structure, lost shrine integrity — corruption exploits your rubble
   The player FEELS both. Destruction sometimes helps, sometimes creates consequences — exactly the principle, now enforceable. The care meter and the wound system are the same machine.

**W5 — Anti-grind is a business-model fight, not a design fight.** Mobile retention pressure will push toward farming loops; design alone won't hold that line without a structural replacement. *The replacement: THE MAP IS THE PROGRESS BAR.* See Progression.

**W6 — "Big HP bar" is the death of the colossal.** World bosses and hunts fail if they're stat-scaled normal enemies. *Fix: the TWO-SCALE BOSS LAW below.*

## DESTRUCTIBLE / REACTIVE WORLD — the Consequence Ledger in play

| INTERACTION | BOON (possible) | WOUND (possible) |
|---|---|---|
| Break a wall | new sprite entrance / giant lane | hollow breach point opens |
| Collapse a bridge | deny an enemy lane | town supply cut — faith regen drops |
| Crush a structure | loot/lore in the rubble | survivor shelter lost |
| Crater the field | chokepoint for armies | your own evacuee route rerouted |
| Element reactions (fire spread, lava rerouting, flood) | area denial | friendly structures burn — care meter |
| Topple trees/towers | area denial, spawn-pit crush | cover lost for sprite content later |

Rules: (1) every destructible is authored and named; (2) every destruction writes Boon OR Wound — never nothing; (3) wounds are repairable by pilgrim-scale gameplay (belief repair, shrine quests) — the god breaks the world, the pilgrim heals it. **That symmetry IS the game.**

## CAMPAIGN MISSION GRAMMAR — variety through the tier system

The 12 mission structures, each mapped to form-pattern grammar (E=explore/sprite, G=glimpse, M=manifestation, A=ascension):

1. **INVESTIGATION** — E only (G optional assist): pure sprite detective work; the discovery IS the climax
2. **RESCUE** — E w/ G: pilgrim-scale extractions, one glimpse to cheat death
3. **ESCORT** — E→M: sprite leads, manifestation clears the blocked pass ahead
4. **HUNT** — E→M: track at pilgrim scale, manifestation fells the quarry
5. **SHRINE RESTORATION** — E (ritual, belief repair)
6. **GATE ACTIVATION** — E→A: the standard pre-ascension mission (charge, rite, cleanliness)
7. **GATE DEFENSE** — M/A: structural waves, mid-fight thinning
8. **CORRUPTION CLEANSING** — M+E: giant purges the field, WARDEN enters the vein-heart inside — the two-scale purge
9. **GIANT MANIFESTATION** — E→M→E: the pure loop mission (Cold Reliquary's middle)
10. **COLOSSAL DEFENSE** — A: protect a colossus under siege (allies CAN be colossi — the mythology's own beings)
11. **BOSS HUNT** — E→A or M: two-scale boss law applies
12. **MULTI-STAGE STORY** — the region climax: E→M→E→A (the Cold Reliquary shape)

**No two consecutive missions share the same form-pattern** — the campaign breathes between forms. **THE COLD RELIQUARY is the benchmark mission:** it runs E→G(bridge)→M(nest)→E(wreckage lore)→A(gate + battlefield + colossus) — every tier, both scales, one map. All new mission types are prototyped against its spine.

## THE TWO-SCALE BOSS LAW (world bosses, colossal hunts, gate-fused bosses)

A colossal boss is NEVER a normal enemy with a big HP bar. Every boss:
- has **body zones / armor parts / limbs** (destructible — Consequence Ledger applies to bosses too)
- has **phases that change the RULES of the arena** (a slam ends phase 1 by re-flooring the battlefield)
- uses **environmental attacks** (its body is a natural disaster — lava bursts, quake rings)
- carries **objectives beyond HP** (protect, escort, close breaches it opens)
- and CRITICALLY: **at least one phase drops to pilgrim scale.** The giant cracks its armor; the WARDEN climbs through the wound to strike the heart; the god-fight and the climb are the same encounter. *God vs God outside, warden vs heart inside.* No other RPG can stage this fight. Every world boss must.
- COLOSSAL HUNTS add a unique mechanic each (armor-break, limb-disable, terrain-luring, settlement-protection, chase, survive-its-destruction) — never an HP sponge.

## AFTER THE CAMPAIGN — three pillars (collapsed from six)

**PILLAR I — THE DEPTHS (dungeons + the Threshold Trials).** Replayable expeditions in the six elemental regions, each with a signature mechanic (fire ruins = burn-timers and ember-light puzzles; frozen temples = ice-bridges the giant's heat melts; storm sanctums = wind-lanes; death crypts = brazier-light vs. darkness; ancient forests = growth/decay cycles; celestial ruins = gravity wells). Structure: Explore → Discover → Fight → Puzzle → MANIFEST → Boss → Reward — every dungeon contains at least one manifestation beat, so dungeons are never "just sprite mode." The Threshold Trials (tower) live here as the gate-interior climb. Mastery-focused: score, rank, first-time lore — not farm.

**PILLAR II — THE INCURSION (Hollow invasions + gate defense challenges).** The living region under progressive corruption: a live campaign board of shifting objectives (defend settlements, close corruption points, rescue, activate gates), the battlefield evolving with your performance, culminating in an ASCENSION against the invasion's source. Gate challenges (stabilize, purify, breach-survive, realm-entry, escape-before-collapse) are Incursion variants — same engine, gate-mythology skins.

**PILLAR III — THE HUNT (world bosses + colossal hunts).** The two-scale boss law applied: each a unique named colossus with a hunt mechanic, arenas in the open regions, on rotation the way storms are — rare, loud, map-shaking.

*Phasing honesty: Pillar I ships first (deepest content reuse), Pillar II second (systems already exist from campaign), Pillar III last (each boss is bespoke).*

## PROGRESSION WITHOUT GRIND — the map is the progress bar

Power never comes from repeating content for numbers. The progression axes:

1. **WORLD-STATE (the primary bar):** region corruption %, gate integrity, shrines restored, settlements thriving, discoveries found. Reclaiming Mythos IS leveling up — visible on the map itself. Post-campaign play drives world-state (an incursion repelled heals a region; a hunt won reopens a road).
2. **RELIC ABILITIES (the build axis):** discoveries and boss-wounds yield relics = NEW VERBS, never stat sticks (a second glimpse slot; a lava-walk; parry-cast; a manifestation extension). Build choices = which verbs you carry into the Depths and Trials.
3. **BELIEF (the social axis):** rescued/settled NPCs build shrines → passive gate integrity — your kindness is literally infrastructure.
4. **COSMETICS & ACHIEVEMENTS:** aspect appearances, shrine art, gate sigils.

**No item levels. No gear score. No re-farming story missions for drop rates.** The economy of "again" is mastery (Trials scoring, Depths routing, hunt mechanics) and world healing — both feed the identity instead of diluting it.

## SYSTEM CHECKLIST — every system reinforcing the scale identity

- **Maps:** walked-first geography, dual-meaning landmarks (Map Architecture) ✦
- **Dungeons:** sprite-depth with mandatory manifestation beats, region signature mechanics ✦
- **Missions:** tier grammar, no repeated form-patterns, Cold Reliquary benchmark ✦
- **Combat:** precision verbs vs. spatial verbs — different interaction sets, one input language ✦
- **Gates:** the tether — all manifestation flows through gate-state ✦
- **Exploration:** discoveries that pay off at the other scale (recon payoff) ✦
- **Bosses:** two-scale boss law — god outside, warden inside ✦
- **Progression:** world-state + relic verbs — the map is the bar ✦
- **Endgame:** three pillars, all gate-flavored, zero grind-farming ✦
- **The line every region must earn:** *"I've been here before — but now I'm seeing it from the perspective of a God."*

## THE CUT LIST (what we are NOT building)
- A third control context (mounts/vehicles) — two scales is the permanent maximum
- Systemic city-destruction simulation — authored destructibles + the Ledger only
- Gear/item-level systems — relic verbs and world-state only
- Six parallel endgame modes — three pillars, phased
- Any feature that serves one scale without pairing the other
