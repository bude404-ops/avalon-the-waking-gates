# F001-AMEKHET — Design Card (New Era)

**Deity:** AMEKHET, the Silent Verdict
**Faction:** F001 Egypt (Sun-Verdict Desert)
**Class:** Assassin / Divine Retribution
**Pipeline:** Stage 1 — Character Lock

## Essence
Khaveth weighs the verdict. Djekhur carries the sentence. Shemris delivers it from afar. AMEKHET is the verdict no one sees coming — the blade that is already there before the crime is finished. She is divine retribution at arm's length: silent, intimate, inescapable. The pantheon's shadow.

## Standing Laws (inherited, all binding)
- Female-Titan Laws 1-5: size parity + feminine shape (Warcraft female-hero logic); voluptuous build (full proud bust, most dramatic curve); NO six-pack abs (smooth classical-goddess torso); unisex over-exaggerated armor; hearts absolute-banned (angular storm-script sigils, max 3)
- Anatomy-fused sandstone (zero separate plates, ornament as relief, gold inlay WITHIN the stone)
- Weathered golden desert sandstone, chiseled facets, cracks, patina
- Grimdark activation: faint ember seams, 3 sigils, ember eyes, no bloom
- Invented divine iconography — NO historical replicas (no nemes, no real cobras/scarabs, no museum pieces)

## Body
- Colossus in the same size tier as Khaveth/Djekhur; athletic toned feminine hourglass
- Where Shemris stands proud in the open, Amekhet reads as coiled readiness — the assassin's stance: weight low, blades crossed low, stillness before the strike
- Darker sandstone patina than the others (the shadow of the pantheon), ember seams slightly brighter in contrast

## Invented Divine Iconography
- NIGHT-BANE VEIL: a hood-like crown of invented crescent-lune geometry sweeping back from her brow like a collar of moons in phases — her own invented crescent forms, not historical
- SERPENTINE STRUT LINES: the gold-inlay linework across her armor flows in swift S-curve invented geometry (the memory of a serpent's path, never a carved cobra replica)
- SHADOW-KEY CHEST RELIEF: layered interlocking invented plate geometry at the sternum, like lock mechanism-craft of the gods
- BRACER SHEATH NETWORKS: carved blade-rest channels along both forearms where her weapons seat when crossed
- WARDEN-GAZE GEM: single carved gemstone at the brow

## Weapon — CRESCENT VEILS (deliberately PAIRED, exactly TWO)
Twin short crescent blades — the assassin's khopesh reimagined as one-of-one divine craft:
- EXACTLY TWO blades — a deliberate matched pair, never one, never three
- Each: a single crescent-moon arc of carved sandstone with gold-inlay strut lines along the outer curve, inner edge bare and brutally sharp
- Grips wrapped in stone cords; no chain, no connection between the two
- Held crossed low before her, points down — the rest position of a verdict waiting

## Stage 1 Composition Rules
- Neutral dais, character-only, full figure, coiled-readiness stance
- Effects as accents; LOTR-cinematic rendering, WoW scale reading
