# DESIGN CARD — KHAVETH (F001, Egypt region)
Status: Stage 1 in progress. TONE LOCKED: GRIMDARK (BudE404, Sept 4). Source of truth for all Khaveth prompts.

## Identity
- **Name:** Khaveth, the Sun-Verdict
- **Faction:** F001 — Sun-Verdict Desert (Egypt-inspired region of Mythos)
- **Class:** Warrior/Judge — god of verdict and noon
- **Weapon:** THE AMBER VERDICT — colossal khopesh of carved golden sandstone with a scale-balanced blade (verdict-scales motif along the spine of the blade), gold linework inlaid, hieroglyph-essence inscription on the blade. Held in right hand, tip planted on the dais.
- **Silhouette:** Mountain-broad shoulders, column neck, monumental chest — the judge who never kneels.

## Material & Body (doctrine: one continuous carved mass)
- Entire body = weathered **golden desert sandstone**, millennia patina, warm godflesh — NO mortal skin tones, chiseled facets everywhere.
- **Armor = anatomy.** No discrete plates. Pectorals swell INTO the breastplate shape; shoulders flow into guard-forms; abdominals carved in layered relief like a war-belt grown from the body. Boundary between body and armor: nonexistent.
- Ornament = **relief carved INTO the stone skin** — fluting, sun-disc motifs, gold inlay as precise linework WITHIN the sandstone (never a separate metal layer).
- **Master-craft design sense:** deliberate, balanced, exquisite divine smithwork grown by the deity's own will.
- **DIVINE ORIGINALITY (locked Sept 4, BudE404): armor and weapons are OUR OWN unique inventions — divine-level, one-of-one, forms no mortal civilization ever made. NO historical replicas, no real hieroglyphs, no nemes headdress. Invented divine geometry only (orbital rings, verdict chevron rays, abstract eye-of-verdict forms).

## Glow
- GRIMDARK **amber-gold** inner light: ominous, wrathful, dangerous — a smoldering sun-heart at the core bleeding through the carved seams and facet cracks like embers under skin. F001 tone = GRIMDARK (locked by BudE404).
- **Ember eyes:** solid glowing amber, casting light onto the cheekbones.
- Sigils (hieroglyph-essence, MAX 3): (1) sun-disc at the heart — largest, brightest; (2) eye-of-verdict glyph on the brow; (3) scale glyph inscribed on the Amber Verdict blade.

## Pose (Stage 1)
- Full figure standing on a plain neutral stone dais — NO world, NO Gate, NO weather, NO mortals.
- Weight shifted, weapon planted, kinetic tension — the judge waking for war, serene but ready.
- Colossal scale reading: 9m+ presence, 10-heads-tall proportions.
