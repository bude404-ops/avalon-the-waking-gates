# vharkar — THE FURNACE WARDEN
## Design Card — Stage 1 Character Lock v1
**Realm:** The Cinderlands (Fire — realm domain of exile)
**Role:** Warden (warrior)
**Weapon:** The Anvilsplit Maul — a great hammer whose head holds a captive sun-core; every strike pours molten judgment
**Armor Philosophy:** CRUCIBLE-PLATE — armor cooled mid-pour: frozen drips, flows, and sag-lines of living metal, as if his plate was poured molten and frozen in the instant of donning

---

## SPEC

**FORMULA:** COLOSSAL x ELEMENTAL x REALM IDENTITY — all three at full strength.

**COLOSSAL (Warcraft Grammar):**
- 10-heads-tall frame, small head, column neck
- Pauldron monuments rising above and sweeping past the head — silhouette's widest point
- Cinder-block hands; armor mass > 1/3 of silhouette
- Weapon scaled to the colossus: the Maul's head is the size of a temple door

**ELEMENTAL (Fire — living):**
- Molten gold-white fire through carved seams, brightest at a captive sun-core in the chest
- Heat shimmer off shoulders and crown; ember particles drifting
- Lower edges of the kilt-carve dissolve into embers and flowing cinder-sand
- EMBER EYES: solid burning gold-white, no pupils, glow touching the cheekbones

**REALM IDENTITY (Cinderlands):**
- Materials: ash-sandstone flesh and plate as one continuous carved mass, obsidian glass accents, molten gold inlay linework
- Crucible-Plate construction: every plate reads as poured-then-frozen — drip-beads at plate edges, sag-lines, flow-bands that never fully hardened
- Crown: a monumental spire crown of stacked tapering forms with obsidian glass shards
- Invented iconography only — no real-culture forms, no artifacts

**WEAPON (function made solid):** THE ANVILSPLIT MAUL — massive two-handed war hammer; the head is a hollow cage of frozen crucible-metal holding a captive sun-core that seethes visibly inside; the haft wrapped in cooled flow-metal bands. He is the Furnace Warden: he keeps the world's fire, and the hammer is the furnace's verdict.

**SIGILS (max 3, brutal angular):** chest chevron (largest, burning within the sun-core), brow chevron, single chevron on the maul head. No letters, no script.

**POSE:** fight-ready planted stance — weight shifted, maul head resting on the dais at his side, both hands on the haft, kinetic tension like he could raise it in the next breath. Stage 1: neutral stone dais, character only.

**RENDERING:** stylized fantasy realism, painterly cinematic quality, his own firelight dominating a neutral warm dais. Text-ban absolute.
