# CLASS TREE — LANTERN (Relic Lantern / Keeper) v1 CANON

> Faction-neutral weapon discipline. Class = hands; god = flame. STAMINA/cooldowns, never FAITH.

## Doctrine
The Relic Lantern is not a weapon — it is a reservoir. The Keeper fights with the FREE HAND (palm bursts, orbs) while the lantern banks and focuses the light. Sustain, control, and a light that never goes out.

## Tier 1 — The Light
1. **RESERVOIR** — Passive. The lantern stores light: +1 charge every 10s, max 3. Charges empower your palm actives.
2. **PALM FLARE** — 20 stamina. Point-blank burst from the open hand; consumes a charge for +100% radius.
3. **STEADY GLOW** — Passive. You and nearby allies regenerate slowly while the lantern is raised.

## Tier 2 — The Focus
4. **LANTERN ARC** — 10s cd. Sweep the lantern: a line of light that blinds enemies 1.5s.
5. **DRAWMOAT** — 25 stamina. Pull the light inward — taunt all enemies in a radius toward you (they hate the flame).
6. **CALM HAND** — Passive. Palm actives cost 20% less stamina while a charge is banked.

## Tier 3 — The Vow
7. **BANKED LIGHT** — 12s cd. Spend all charges: heal 8 HP per charge to yourself and allies in the glow.
8. **WARDING GLOW** — 30 stamina. The lantern flares into a dome: allies inside take 20% less damage for 5s.
9. **UNSHAKEN CARRY** — Passive. While the lantern is raised you resist knockback and fear.

## Tier 4 — Capstone (mastery, not god)
10. **THE KEEPER'S VIGIL** — 25s cooldown. Plant the lantern: it becomes a beacon for 10s — constant ally regen, enemies in its wide glow are slowed, and your palm actives are free while you stand in the light. Move away and it returns to your hand.

## Stat pins (engine source of truth)
- Palm base: 12 dmg burst. Charge timer 10s, max 3.
- Stamina pool 100. Lantern glow radius 6m.
- Synergy: the faction palm/cone rites (Furnace Palm, Wisp Palm) share the free-hand grammar — visual language already canonized.
