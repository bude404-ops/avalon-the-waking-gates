# AVALON REGION CONTENT SYSTEM — Six Realms, Six Content Skins

**Big's law (Sept 10, 2026):** maps must carry the lore and storyline. Each class
starts in its own home region (Origin Law v2), so each region needs its own
quests, stories, dialogs, NPCs, and dungeons — distinct per region. Never
copy-paste content between realms.

**The design rule:** ONE structural loop, SIX content skins. The Gate Rite
pilgrimage (arrival in a wounded land → pilgrimage → relight the Gates → the
Rite wakes the god → realm battle → healed land) is the shared SPINE — the
doctrine stays one. Everything the player touches inside that spine — who they
meet, what they're asked to do, what the dungeons are, how people talk — is
region-native. A quest may never move between realms without a full rewrite.

## PER-REGION CONTENT STACK (the deliverable shape, ×6)

Every region bible contains the same nine sections — filled only with that
region's material:

1. **ORIGIN ARC** — the class-specific opening. The Sovereign's first hour in
   Skyrend ≠ the Ravager's first hour in Ashfall. The Marked dream (doctrine:
   the god's dream settles on the person whose life matches the realm's need)
   lands differently per class/realm pair. Opening quest, opening NPC, first
   Gate glimpse, first Hollow contact.
2. **MAIN QUESTLINE** — the Gate Rite pilgrimage in region-native beats:
   the wounded-land arrival, the Gate relighting chain, the Rite. Named beats,
   each a 3–6 minute mission per the Beat Discipline.
3. **SIDE QUESTS** — drawn from the nine quest categories (Kingdom,
   Exploration, Primordial, Class, Divine, Relic, Monster, Mystery, Choice),
   each with a region-native example set.
4. **NPCS** — from the roster section + new named locals (name pools), each
   with role, want, and quest hook. The Ravenkin Luminary-bearers cross realms.
5. **DIALOG STYLE GUIDE** — per-region voice: Ashfall speaks in forge-oaths and
   heat idioms; Skyrend in wind-readings and watch-vows; Stoneheart in
   stone-patience and ledger-keeping; Duskmourn in barrow-hush and oath-wary
   double meanings; Marenth in tide-songs and salt-speech; Everbloom in
   grow-speak and wild-kinship. NO modern slang anywhere, per lore law.
6. **DUNGEON(S)** — one-time authored adventures (anti-grind law), region-
   native: e.g. Ashfall = the sealed forge-vault under the caldera; Skyrend =
   the wind-hollowed watchtower interior; Stoneheart = a glyph-vein mine that
   dug too deep; Duskmourn = a barrow-crypt of the Oathless; Marenth = a
   drowned bell-tower walked at low tide; Everbloom = a strangler-heart grove
   where the wild turned wrong. One-shot each, replay optional, never farming.
7. **ENEMIES** — realm Hollow variants + region fauna (CAMELOT_BESTIARY,
   AVALON_CREATURES): neutral staging laws hold for canon art.
8. **WORLD-CHANGE MOMENTS** — deterministic set-pieces (doctrine 2.5), region-
   native consequences the player authors by action.
9. **TWO-SCALE PAYOFF MAP** — the landmarks the player will later hold at god
   scale (Landmark Memory): name the shrine, the tower, the road they'll
   grieve/weaponize.

## ROLLOUT ORDER (verdict-gated, like all content)

1. **ASHFALL FIRST** — it pairs with the Cold Lantern map (Map #1) and the
   Ravager (first pipeline class): one region's full stack ships as the
   template. Ashfall's Origin Arc = the Ravager's opening (the class whose
   home this is) while the Cold Lantern mission remains the design-test
   pilgrimage ANY pilgrim can walk.
2. Then per vertical-slice need: Skyrend (Sovereign pair), then the rest by
   realm-bible queue (Big can reorder anytime).
3. Each region bible rolls section-by-section with Big's verdicts — same
   protocol as canon art. Nothing locks without his Keep.

## LAWS THAT BIND ALL SIX (unchanged)

Anti-grind (no dailies/farming/FOMO), one-time dungeons, permanent world
changes, cosmetic-only monetization, the Two Scales, MYTHIC WORLD LAW staging,
no deity names/titles or 'elven' in generation prompts, runes only where canon
allows (gate-rune the sole mark), Lantern-Region Aura colorways, and the
class↔realm binding fixed per Origin Law v2.
