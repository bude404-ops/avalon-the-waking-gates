# TEASER v3 — BIG'S OWN VOICE CUT (Sept 11 2026)
Written for MC to record himself. Short lines, natural pauses marked with "...", no tongue-twisters, whisper finale. Fits the v3 art sequence (mountain → traveler → reliquary kindle → six → gate → title). Target read: slow, low, unhurried — a man telling an old story by a fire.

## SCRIPT 1 — "THE PROMISE" (full cut, ~40-45s of VO over 79s teaser)

[BEAT 1 — over the long road / mountain — slow, low]
Before kings... there was the mountain.

[BEAT 2 — the gate reveal]
And beneath it... a gate. Older than any name we still remember.

[BEAT 3 — the reliquary kindles — almost reverent]
The old world kept one flame... and gave it a vessel.

[BEAT 4 — the realms]
Six realms hold the seals. Six thrones sleep.

[BEAT 5 — the crack — let this line land cold]
But every seal cracks. And everything buried... wakes.

[BEAT 6 — the six — one beat per class shot, no rush]
Six will answer the road.
The storm. The ash. The stone.
The dusk. The tide. The bloom.

[BEAT 7 — near silence, slower than feels comfortable]
One flame. One vessel. One chance... to carry it home.

[BEAT 8 — over black, whispered]
AVALON. The gates are waking.

## SCRIPT 2 — "THE SHORT DARK" (minimal cut, ~20-25s, if he wants leaner)

The world was built on a promise...
and the promise is breaking.

Six realms. Six seals. One flame left to carry.

Some roads you walk.
Some roads... walk you.

[whisper] AVALON.

## RECORDING TIPS (no studio needed)
- Phone mic works: quiet room, 6-8 inches away, slightly off-axis so breath doesn't pop.
- Read it TWICE as slow as feels natural — then slow that down once more. The cold is in the pacing.
- The "..." are real pauses. Let the silence carry weight; the teaser sound design fills around him.
- Beat 8 is a true whisper — drop volume, keep the air.
- Send me the raw take(s) — even as a Telegram voice note. I'll clean it (noise floor, EQ, hall), cut to the beats, and build the full mix under it.
- Either script, or mix lines from both — his call. If he records both, I cut the strongest.
